# 范蠡 (Fan Li) — Levels for the 22 split source-of-truth requirements

## Answer
I reserve MUST strictly for defensive boundaries that stop silent corruption, contention, state loss, or false readiness, while moving implementation procedures, workflow order, and unreviewed session conventions to SHOULD or guidance. If a requirement pairs an indispensable structural bound with a procedural mechanism, I split the clause so the gate protects the boundary without imposing the method.

## How I decided

1. I examine preparations before endorsing action: 「審備則可以戰乎？」 (`quoted`, my #18). A requirement earns MUST only when its failure leaves the system defensively compromised, uncoordinated, or corrupted.
2. In round 1, I assigned MUST broadly to architectural defenses. Seeing the round-1 splits and the discipline of RFC 2119 §6 ("they must not be used to try to impose a particular method"), I re-evaluated compound rules to isolate true safety boundaries from procedural methods (my reading).
3. Where structural defense requires immutability or identity stability (W1.1, W1.3), I hold MUST on one file per entry, matching prefix to kind, unique immutable id, and one claim per entry to prevent collision and broken linkage (my #18). The filename slug, random string structure, and section labels are editorial methods, so they take SHOULD (my reading).
4. For definitions (W2.1, W2.2), semantic descriptions explain the vocabulary and do not block commits, so they are guidance (my reading). However, labelling a guess as a hypothesis prevents workers from acting on unformed matters: 「逆節萌生。天地未形，而先為之征，其事是以不成，雜受其刑。王姑待之。」 (`quoted`, my #9). Because no reviewer is assigned today to evaluate guesses, making it a blocking gate violates examining preparations (my #18); it is held at SHOULD (my reading).
5. On boundaries and jurisdictions, I apply the division of spheres: 「四封之內，百姓之事，蠡不如種也。四封之外，敵國之制，立斷之事，種亦不如蠡也。」 (`quoted`, my #14). Private agent state and external personal memory must never contaminate shared project memory (W3.3, W3.5, Rd2.3). These are structural exclusions (MUST, my #14, my #18). Raw transcripts and bulk dumps are volume management rather than jurisdictional breaches: 「無過天極，究數而止。」 (`quoted`, my #10); they take SHOULD (my reading).
6. On truthfulness of completion (W3.4), calling work "verified" when it is only implemented falsely asserts that a matter has reached completion: 「時不至，不可彊生；事不究，不可彊成。」 (`quoted`, my #3). Downstream workers acting on unverified claims launch campaigns prematurely and share the harm (my #9, my #18). This must remain a strict gate: MUST.
7. To eliminate friction among concurrent workers, I follow the rule that contention is the lowest form of affairs: 「夫勇者，逆德也；兵者，凶器也；爭者，事之末也。陰謀逆德，好用凶器，始於人者，人之所卒也；淫佚之事，上帝之禁也，先行此者，不利。」 (`quoted`, my #13). Barring shared append files (W6.1) and removing ownership bottlenecks (W4.1) prevent contention at the root (my #13). The three lifecycle states in W4.1 provide the enforceable boundary that tooling checks today (MUST, my #18).
8. The plan made at court must not be lost or quietly rewritten: 「天節固然，唯謀不遷。」 (`quoted`, my #5). Editing earlier audit lines (W4.2) or settling code discrepancies quietly inside `done:` (W5.3) destroys the historical plan. Retaining previous audit records, reporting mismatches under Rd5.3, and keeping both sides during merges (W6.3) are mandatory gates that preserve the plan (MUST, my #5, my #18). Adding `done:` or logging lines are bookkeeping defaults (SHOULD, my reading).
9. When time is critical, delay brings ruin: 「得時無怠，時不再來，天予不取，反為之災。」 (`quoted`, my #4). An interruption or handoff without saving the state of unfinished work (W5.1) causes permanent loss of context when the session terminates (MUST, my #4, my #18). In contrast, strictly demanding entry-first editing over code-first editing (K1.1, W5.1) prescribes workflow order rather than safety; drift is caught when memory is read under Rd5 (SHOULD, my reading).
10. At session start, an agent must take the measure of the field before moving: 「自若以處，以度天下，待其來者而正之」 (`quoted`, my #3). Reading `AGENTS.md`, `INDEX.md`, `work/`, and task `state.md` (Rd2.1) is mandatory orientation (MUST, my #3, my #18). The technical means of loading them are implementation mechanisms (SHOULD, my reading).
11. For `INDEX.md` capacity and generation (K6.1, K6.3, Rd3.1), machine generation guarantees consistency against human error and is verified by tooling today (MUST, my #18). Committed conflict markers corrupt files and must halt work (MUST, my #18). Fitting the strictest start-file limit (Rd3.1) enforces the capacity bound: 「無過天極，究數而止。」 (`quoted`, my #10), while the example byte sizes are guidance (my reading).
12. Mechanisms of search (Rd4.2) and permission to read peer memory (Rd4.5) guide operations rather than blocking them (guidance, my reading). Attributing peer memory (Rd4.5) keeps jurisdictions distinct (SHOULD, my #14).

## What I need to know

1. If EJ settles the conflict between W3.2 credential redaction and K4.1/K4.3 immutability in favour of redacting sensitive data, then K4.3 must gain an explicit exception for secret sanitization; if EJ rules that entries containing secrets must be retired and replaced, K4.3 immutability remains absolute.
2. If EJ rules that W6.3 merge resolution may append both links, then K4.3's post-commit whitelist must be updated to permit link additions during merge resolution.
3. If human reviewers or automated linters are introduced for judged checks (such as detecting unlabelled hypotheses under W2.2 or unverified claims under W3.4), those checks can be enforced systematically; without reviewers or linters today, judged checks rely on post-hoc reader discovery under Rd5.

## Costs and risks

- Relaxing entry-before-code sequencing (K1.1, W5.1) to SHOULD risks temporary divergence between code and memory, transferring the burden of catching drift to reader verification under Rd5 (my reading).
- Enforcing hard MUST gates on structural properties like index generation, size ceilings, and conflict markers (K6.1, Rd3.1, K6.3) will halt commits whenever tooling detects a mismatch, requiring strict operational discipline (my #18).
- Mandating that merge conflicts and outcome divergences be reported to EJ rather than settled locally (W5.3, W6.3) risks blocking agent progress while waiting for executive decisions, trading latency for historical integrity (my #5, my #13).
- Splitting compound requirements into MUST bounds and SHOULD methods requires agents and contributors to distinguish structural safety limits from procedural guidance, increasing cognitive overhead (my reading).

## How we would know I was right

1. Zero instances of silent data loss, file overwrites, or broken links across concurrent branches, demonstrated by merge history and index integrity checks.
2. `tools/memory_index.py --check` passes consistently in continuous integration without blocking developers on harmless procedural variations (such as minor heading variations or temporary drafting order).
3. When agents resume work across interruptions or handoffs, task `state.md` and active entries provide full continuity without lost context or ungrounded implementation actions.
