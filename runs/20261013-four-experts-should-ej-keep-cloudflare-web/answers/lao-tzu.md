# 老子 (Laozi) — keep Cloudflare analytics on ancients.ejai.ai?

## Answer

Turn it off for this page only: one exclude rule for this hostname, N2 left exactly as it is, and no rewriting of the requirement. If you ever do want to know who reads that page, name the purpose in words first — then keep it (my #12).

## How I decided

1. **My #1 (ch. 48) — the page's own rule is subtraction.** "Learning adds something every day; the Way takes something away every day… Lose and lose again, until one arrives at not-doing." N2 is that rule already written down. Analytics is not a defect being repaired; it is one more thing added to a page whose whole design was refusal of addition.
2. **My #13 (chs. 30, 31) — the instrument's own condition is not met.** "Weapons are instruments of ill omen… when it cannot be avoided he uses them." There *is* an alternative here, and it is one line long. The words reserve the instrument for the case where there is no alternative; this is not that case. Also "The good one has a result and that is all; he does not dare use it to take strength" — you have stated no plan to read the numbers, so the data would be gathered for nothing and displayed to nobody.
3. **My #5 (chs. 57, 37) — this is a matter with no business in it.** "Take the world with no business… I have no business, and the people enrich themselves." No login, no forms, nothing sold, no plan to look at the counts. That is the shape of 無事, not the shape of a thing that needs watching.
4. **My #3 (ch. 9) — stop before it is full.** "To hold it and fill it is not as good as stopping." One zone-wide rule since 2026-01-22 is the full vessel: every page added from now on is filled automatically. The single exclude is the stopping, and it is the cheapest stopping available.
5. **My #2 (chs. 44, 46) and my #16 (ch. 33) — enough, and stop.** "Knowing what is enough, one is not disgraced; knowing to stop, one is not endangered… one who knows what is enough is rich." *My reading, not the words:* nothing on this page turns on your knowing who opened it, so the measuring is not a gain, and the count of things the page asks of a reader's phone stays at one (a font) rather than two.
6. **My #17 (ch. 11) — and I mark this as mine.** "Thirty spokes share one hub; in its nothing lies the cart's use… what is not there makes for use." My item itself records that "The words do not say to remove anything; that would be an application." So I claim only that the page's use lives in what it does not carry — and the empty space is the part a reader actually reads. I do not claim the text commands you to remove anything.
7. **My #12 (ch. 36) — the counterweight, and the reason the answer is conditional.** "About to abolish it, one must first raise it… about to seize it, one must first give to it." If this page will ever need numbers, the order is: write the need down first, *then* abolish the script. Today the need does not exist, so the step does not fire. It is the step that fires later.
8. **My #14 (ch. 22) — count.** "Little, then gained; much, then confused." Each subdomain that quietly carries a second script into a page that promised one file is 多. It is one exception today.
9. **My #6 (ch. 64) — do it while it is easy, and treat it as difficult.** "Act on it before it exists; order it before it is in disorder." The exclude is one line, now, before the page has other readers. *My reading:* removing the script after the page has been shared and has an audience is no longer 治之於未亂. And "much taken as easy must bring much difficulty" — the cheap step is also the one that hardens.

## What I need to know

- **Do you intend, at any point, to look at this page's numbers?** If yes, my answer flips to keep it — that is exactly my #12, and the requirement would then be honestly widened rather than quietly broken.
- **Will the page be shared, and will you want to know anything about those readers?** My list does not settle this: measuring one's own page and measuring other people's reading are different acts, and my reading is that only the first is a matter of not-doing.
- **Is there another way to know the page still loads on your phone?** If not, the removal of the script is the removal of a signal, not just of a request.
- **Does one hostname exclude hold on its own, or must each new subdomain be re-decided by hand?** The second is a standing tax and it decays.
- **What is N2 protecting — the page's promise, or the reader's privacy?** You told me it forbids network requests apart from Google Fonts, so on the words given analytics breaks it. If N2 only ever meant "no third-party tracking", this is a different question.

## Costs and risks

- **Cost of the exclude:** ancients.ejai.ai's traffic becomes invisible, and a page that fails on a phone fails quietly (my reading; it is the price my #13 pays for taking away the instrument in a case where the instrument was available).
- **Cost of the exclude:** an exception list is where drift begins — one hostname today, four in a year, none of them remembered as decisions. "To hold it and fill it is not as good as stopping" (my #3).
- **Cost of keeping it:** N2 changes from "one file, no tracking" to something that admits a Cloudflare script, and the written requirement becomes weaker for whoever checks it later. "Little, then gained; much, then confused" (my #14). If the page is shared, the promise has been weakened before anyone else read it.
- **Cost of my own seat:** the items I leaned on hardest are the later ones. My #10, #12, #14, #16 and #17 have no Guodian witness, and my #18 and #19 are `attributed` — my #18 comes through Sima Qian, who offers three candidates and writes世莫知其然否, and my #19 is the 天下 author's description of 老耼. These are the received text's rules, not the earliest ones (my reading).
