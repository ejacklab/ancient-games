## Gaps
- **Guodian Bamboo Slips textual readings:** The local source repository contains no Guodian transcription file (only Wang Bi, Mawangdui silk text, and Shiji 63). Guodian claims regarding chapter presence (e.g. Murphy 2006 table) could not be cross-checked against Guodian primary readings directly.
- **Excluded ungrounded extrapolations:** Passages like ch. 60 (治大國若烹小鮮) and ch. 5 (多言數窮，不如守中 / 多聞數窮) express concise proverbs, but framing them as operational decision procedures without adding modern instructional scaffolding proved impossible; they are left out rather than supplemented.
- **Unverifiable historical individual:** As recorded in Shiji 63, the historical author cannot be independently isolated from the compiled textual corpus of the Daodejing; hence items 1–11 and 14–17 reflect the received and excavated texts (`quoted`), while item 13 reflects Sima Qian's narrative record of speech (`attributed`). No birth dates, biographical assertions, or unverified traditions were introduced.
