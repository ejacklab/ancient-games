1. **Kind:** decision rule
   **Rule:** Matters of state consist of holding the full, steadying the leaning, and ordering affairs; each corresponds to Heaven, people, and Earth. When Heaven's timing does not arise, do not act as the attacker; when human conditions do not arise, do not initiate action.
   **Their words:** 「夫國家之事，有持盈，有定傾，有節事。」/「持盈者與天，定傾者與人，節事者與地。」/「天時不作，弗為人客；人事不起，弗為之始。」 — "Matters of state have holding the full, steadying the leaning, ordering affairs." / "Holding the full accords with Heaven; steadying the leaning accords with people; ordering affairs accords with Earth." / "When Heaven's timing does not arise, do not be the guest [attacker]; when human affairs do not arise, do not initiate."
   **Source:** 國語·卷21 越語下 §1 (Wikisource 國語/卷21; parallel in 史記·卷041).

2. **Kind:** decision rule
   **Rule:** When the season has not arrived, do not force growth; when affairs have not run their course, do not force completion. Remain composed, gauge all under heaven, keep the people productively employed without idleness, and if no opening or seasonal reversal occurs, protect and instruct the people while awaiting it.
   **Their words:** 「時不至，不可彊生；事不究，不可彊成。自若以處，以度天下，待其來者而正之，因時之所宜而定之。同男女之功，除民之害，以避天殃。田野開闢，府倉實，民眾殷。無曠其眾，以為亂梯。…事無閒，時無反，則撫民保教以須之。」 — "When the season has not arrived, growth cannot be forced; when affairs have not run their course, completion cannot be forced. Remain composed in place to gauge the world; wait for what comes and align with it; establish matters according to what suits the season. ...Leave not the multitude idle, lest it become the ladder of disorder. ...If affairs present no fissure and the season offers no reversal, then nurture the people and safeguard instruction to await it."
   **Source:** 國語·卷21 越語下 §1.
   **Words are:** `quoted`
   **From:** top-12 #2

Plotting secretly is contrary to virtue; fondness for lethal instruments and initiating against others ends in being destroyed by others; debauchery is what the Lord on High forbids; to practice these first brings no advantage."

## Gaps
- **Attribution boundary of economic doctrine:** The famous commercial rules in 史記·卷129 (「旱則資舟，水則資車」, 「貴上極則反賤，賤下極則反貴」, 「財幣欲其行如流水」) are spoken by Fan Li's teacher Ji Ran (計然), not by Fan Li. The biographical narrative descriptions of Fan Li's trading at Tao (「與時逐而不責於人。故善治生者，能擇人而任時」, 「十九年之中三致千金，再分散與貧交疏昆弟」) represent Sima Qian's narrative voice, not Fan Li's spoken decision rules. Fan Li's authentic spoken rule regarding commercial practice is strictly his decision to transfer Ji Ran's five proven state policies to his private household (#16).
- **Proverbs excluded:** The proverb 「千金之子，不死於市」 cited in 史記·卷041 and 史記·卷129 is explicitly introduced as a popular saying (「吾聞千金之子不死於市」 / 「諺曰」), not Fan Li's own coined rule.
- **External and late texts uncheckable:** Texts such as 越絕書, 吳越春秋, 會稽典錄 (cited in 史記正義), 無能子, and 春秋戰國異辭 were referenced in secondary commentaries but are absent from the local repository (`runs/20261013-four-seats/sources/fan-li/`). In accordance with the brief's instruction, no quotes from these unverified witnesses were promoted to the final list.
- **Wikitext commentary reconciliation:** In 國語·卷21 §1, the phrase 「時不至，不可彊生；事不究，不可彊成」 appears in 韋昭's inline commentary on line 3, but also appears verbatim in Fan Li's main spoken speech on line 9; it is retained as authentic spoken text based on line 9.
