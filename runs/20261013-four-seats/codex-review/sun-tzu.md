## Claims elsewhere in the file that the sources contradict

- **Review #4:** “The received text does not say ‘be quick’” overlooks “故兵聞拙速，未睹巧之久也” and “故兵之情主速.” Speed has conditions and costs, but is expressly advocated. 軍爭 is also broader than march speed: it covers deception, signals, morale and attack restrictions. 作戰 [line 21](/home/smoke01/dev/ancient-games/runs/20261013-four-seats/sources/sun-tzu/孫子兵法.txt:21), 九地 [line 140](/home/smoke01/dev/ancient-games/runs/20261013-four-seats/sources/sun-tzu/孫子兵法.txt:140), 軍爭 [lines 96–102](/home/smoke01/dev/ancient-games/runs/20261013-four-seats/sources/sun-tzu/孫子兵法.txt:96).
- **Review #8:** “Every virtue is a lever against you” generalizes five specified dangers into all virtues. The source explicitly calls these five “將之過.” 九變 [line 111](/home/smoke01/dev/ancient-games/runs/20261013-four-seats/sources/sun-tzu/孫子兵法.txt:111).
- **Review #10:** Uncompleted victory being “worse than a plan that failed” is absent; the passage calls it 凶 without that comparison. 火攻 [line 159](/home/smoke01/dev/ancient-games/runs/20261013-four-seats/sources/sun-tzu/孫子兵法.txt:159).
- **What the top 10 missed:** “You are protected for it” exceeds “退不避罪,” which says the general does not avoid blame or punishment when retreating. “Morale … is a function of time since the last engagement” introduces an elapsed-time variable absent from 朝／晝／暮. 地形 [line 131](/home/smoke01/dev/ancient-games/runs/20261013-four-seats/sources/sun-tzu/孫子兵法.txt:131), 軍爭 [line 100](/home/smoke01/dev/ancient-games/runs/20261013-four-seats/sources/sun-tzu/孫子兵法.txt:100).
