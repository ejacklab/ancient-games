# Four seats — design under review

Status: proposed, 2026-10-13. Written by Claude (main session) from a conversation with EJ. Not accepted.

## The problem it answers (EJ's words, quoted)

1. "an llm AI suggestion doesn't seem to have a logic most of the time"
2. "logic is 1+1 = 2 and a lot common sense"
3. "many things on earth is not 100 correct or wrong, many things can have more than 1 option"
4. "so the objective and vision and requirements are important"
5. "it is very hard to have all the small requriements write out , else will be like few Terabyte even for a small app"
6. "actually this is why I have the ancient game"
7. "I feel I want 3 person help me" → "Linus Torvalds, 老子 and 孫子" → "maybe 范蠡 better" → "or maybe we can have 4"

## Claude's reading of the problem (my reading, not measured)

- A suggestion is logical when its **facts** plus its **stated common sense** add up to it, as plainly as 1+1=2.
- AI suggestions fail in two ways: they skip the step (state the result without the 1s), or they use generic world
  common sense instead of this project's.
- With several reasonable options, the **objective** decides. Without a written objective the AI quietly picks its
  own goal, and the suggestion looks illogical.
- Requirements cannot all be written. Write only where the AI's default differs from EJ's; let the list grow from
  surprises; ask only on big or irreversible choices.

## The four seats

| Seat | Question it answers | Principle it is built from (quoted) |
|---|---|---|
| 范蠡 | What is the goal, is it the right time, what does it cost, when do we stop? | 時不至，不可強生；事不究，不可強成 (國語·越語下); 蜚鳥盡，良弓藏 (史記·越王勾踐世家) |
| 孫子 | How would this fail? Where does the opponent — or reality — attack? | 兵者，詭道也; 知彼知己，百戰不殆 |
| 老子 | What can we remove? | 為學日益，為道日損 (ch.48); 有無相生 (ch.2) |
| Linus Torvalds | Show me: does it add up, where is the evidence? | "Talk is cheap. Show me the code." |

- **Order:** 范蠡 → 孫子 → 老子 → Linus, sequential, one shared state file.
- **Judge:** EJ. When two options are both logical, the value choice goes back to EJ. No voting.
- **How many seats a task gets:** 范蠡 decides — a small task may need only Linus; all four for big or irreversible
  decisions.
- **Guard against style over judgment:** each seat is built from its person's written principles plus a concrete
  question, never "act like X". Known weakness: 范蠡 left no book of his own; he is known through 國語 and 史記.
- **Distinct jobs:** 孫子 attacks the plan from outside; Linus checks the plan's own claims. 老子 makes it smaller;
  孫子 makes it tougher.
