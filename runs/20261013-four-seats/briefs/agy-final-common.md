# agy brief — research and review, final list of at most 20

You are working for EJ's Ancient Games project on ONE person (named in your seat brief). Two engines have already
worked on this person. Your job is to do your own research, review their work, and produce the person's final list of
**mindsets, decision rules and algorithms — at most 20**. Read-only: write no files; your final message is the answer.

## Read first (all under runs/20261013-four-seats/)
1. `identity/<seat>.md` — first research (opencode): who they are, top 10.
2. `top12/<seat>.md` — reviewed top 12 (opencode).
3. `codex-review/<seat>.md` — a second engine's check of the top 12 against the source texts. **Take this seriously:**
   it found that most rules add things the quoted words do not say (an order of steps, "never", "only", extra
   conditions), and some reverse the meaning. `codex-review/summary.md` has the counts.
4. `sources/<seat>/` — the source texts (see `sources/README.md`; in Chinese wikitext `{{*|…}}` is a commentator's
   note, not the main text).

Then research on the web for anything important the lists miss or get wrong. Prefer primary sources.

## The one rule that matters most
**A rule may say only what its words say.** Write each rule as a close plain-English reading of the quoted words. Do
not add steps, an order, "never", "only", "always", a count, a condition or a modern application that the words do
not contain. If an item cannot be stated without adding something, it is not ready — leave it out or list it in Gaps.

## "At most 20" means a cap, not a target
Keep the important ones. Fill beyond 12 only with items that are important for how this person decided, well sourced,
and not a duplicate of another item. Fewer than 20 is normal.

## Write exactly this (Markdown, no preamble)

# <Name> — final list

## Changes from the top 12
One line per top-12 item: **kept / corrected / merged into #n / dropped** and why. Cite the codex row when you use it.
Where you disagree with codex, say so and show the source text.

## Final list
Numbered, most important first. For each:
- **Kind:** mindset · decision rule · algorithm (algorithm only if the source itself gives steps or an order)
- **Rule:** one or two sentences, saying only what the words say.
- **Their words:** original text in its original language, plus an English gloss.
- **Source:** precise enough to find (book + chapter, or document + section, or URL).
- **Words are:** `quoted` (this person's own words) · `attributed` (recorded about them by someone else, or a
  narrator's voice) — say which.
- **From:** `top-12 #n` or `new (your research)`.

## Gaps
What you could not source, what you left out because it could not be stated without adding to the words, and any
source you could not open. **Do not invent a quote or round a paraphrase up to a quote.**
