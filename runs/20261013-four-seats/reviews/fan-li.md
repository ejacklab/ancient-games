## Findings
1. Lines 5–23 — The problem is stated in EJ's words, but the design gives no goal for itself: nothing says what result would show the four seats worked. It fails its own line 20: "Without a written objective the AI quietly picks its own goal". — principle: 事不究，不可強成 ("if the matter is not worked through, it cannot be forced to completion", 國語·越語下) — label: my reading
2. Lines 35–36 — "a small task may need only Linus", but 范蠡 has to run to decide that, so the smallest review is two seats. No rule says how 范蠡 tells "small" from "big", so that choice is unchecked. — principle: 時不至，不可強生 ("if the time has not come, do not force it into being") — label: my reading
3. Line 3 and lines 38–39 — Now is the time to try it, not to build on it. The problem is live (lines 7–13), and this run is the first use. At n=0, nothing yet shows the seats give different, useful findings. — principle: 得時無怠，時不再來 ("having the moment, do not slacken; it does not come twice", 國語·越語下) — label: my reading
4. Line 34 — No cost is stated, and nothing says what the seats save or what they are compared with. Four passes cost more than one; whether they catch more is unknown. — principle: 事不究，不可強成 — label: not measured
5. Lines 34–41 — There is no stop condition. Nothing says whether a review ends after one pass, or whether the seats run again when a finding changes the plan, or how many times. Nothing says when to retire a seat that stops finding anything, or the whole mechanism. — principle: 蜚鳥盡，良弓藏 ("when the birds are gone, the good bow is put away", 史記·越王勾踐世家) — label: my reading

## Verdict
The timing is right for a trial, but the design fails this seat's question: it has no goal of its own, no cost against a comparison, and no stop condition, either for a review or for the seats.
