# 范蠡 (Fan Li) — top 12, reviewed

## Review of the top 10

1. **Change.** This is two rules welded together. The first (two gates) is really the first two channels of rule 5 — 天時 and 人事 *are* 與天 and 與人. The second (the 申胥 audience) is a different rule entirely: a sign of decay is not yet the decay. Keep only the first here and split the second out; both are promoted below, and the whole framework is merged into one algorithm at #1.
2. **Keep, extend.** The right rule, but it stops half a step. 國語 gives the other half immediately: 「得時不成，天有還形。天節不遠，五年復反，小凶則近，大凶則遠」 — if you get the moment and fail to finish, Heaven reverses and the window returns within five years. Merged in at #4. Also one factual slip: 玄月 is the **ninth** month (爾雅曰：「九月為玄」, and 韋昭 dates it to 魯哀十六年九月), not "the last month of the year."
3. **Change.** Right rule, sloppy quote. 「唯謀不遷」 quoted alone loses its subject; the actual clause is 「天節固然，唯謀不遷」 — Heaven's season is solid, *only the plan* does not shift. The contrast is the argument. Also 「夫十年謀之，一朝而棄之」 is the same rule a second time; one quote is enough. Promoted to #6.
4. **Keep, fix the quote.** The quote silently splices: the source reads 「…始於人者，人之所卒也；**淫佚之事，上帝之禁也，**先行此者，不利。」 The dropped clause is a separate charge (debauchery is forbidden by Heaven), not part of the "whoever begins it is destroyed by it" argument, and the cut is unmarked. The 史記 parallel 「試身於所末，上帝禁之，行者不利」 has no such gap. Promoted to #7.
5. **Change — merged into #1.** As written it is only the diagnostic half. Its own words 「有持盈，有定傾，有節事」 are immediately answered by 「持盈者與天，定傾者與人，節事者與地」, and then the *decision* half follows: 「天時不作，弗為人客；人事不起，弗為之始」. One framework, not two rules. The "How it decides" line is also the best in the top 10 — it shows the three answers being produced in order.
6. **Keep.** Clean, single-trigger, and correctly kept in 國語's wording rather than reconciled with 史記's. Promoted to #9.
7. **Change — this is the biggest rewrite.** It kept the labels and threw away the method. The passage says 「**用人無藝，往從其所**」 — 韋昭 glosses this as 「因敵為制，不豫設也」, *do not pre-set the method; take it from the enemy*. The top 10 dropped that clause and kept only 後則用陰／宜為人客, which are the labels, not the rule. It also dropped the three checks that actually gate an engagement (「必因天地之災」「又觀其民之飢飽勞逸以參之」「盡其陽節、盈吾陰節而奪之」) and the battlefield instruction 「彼來從我，固守勿與」. Rewritten at #8.
8. **Keep.** Best-shaped rule in the set: one trigger, one action, and he names his own weakness in his own mouth. Promoted to #10.
9. **Change — and it is a mindset, not a decision rule.** Three different moments merged: the warning letter (misjudge the ruler), 「君行令，臣行意」 (the servant's own will), and the Qi resignation (a commoner has reached his ceiling). They are one posture, but the top 10 never states the *reason* he gives first — 「今事已濟矣，蠡請從會稽之罰」: the debt was incurred in hardship and is discharged when the work is done. Also a sourcing defect: 「君行**令**，臣行意」 is 史記/卷41. 國語/卷21 reads 「君行**制**，臣行意」 (韋昭: 制，法也；意，志也). The source line lists both under one heading, so the string checks out in 史記 and the error is invisible. Rewritten as one mindset at #5.
10. **Drop from the top 12, keep in the file.** This is the weakest-attributed rule of the ten: none of it is in his voice. 「與時逐而不責於人」 and 「十九年之中三致千金」 are 史記's narrator in 貨殖列傳; the doctrine itself is 計然's (「知斗則修備，時用則知物…財幣欲其行如流水」), and the research says so correctly in its Gaps. 「此天下之中，交易有無之路通」 is also 史記's narrator. Nothing here is a *decision* he is recorded making — it is a biography's judgement of a career. If the seat ever needs commerce rules, re-promote 「與時逐而不責於人。故善治生者，能擇人而任時」 (史記/卷129) as a personnel rule: choose the people, then use the timing.

## What the top 10 missed

- **What you do while you wait.** The top 10 says "wait" six times and never once says what to *do* during the wait. 國語 answers it directly: 「時不至，不可彊生；事不究，不可彊成。**自若以處，以度天下，待其來者而正之，因時之所宜而定之。**…**事無閒，時無反，則撫民保教以須之**」 — make no forced move, measure everything, fix what the season fixes, and if no turn comes, govern and protect the people until it does. The research noticed 「無曠其眾，以為亂梯」 and filed it in Gaps; it is half of a larger answer it did not see. Promoted to #2 — for a seat whose whole job is "is it the right time?", this is the most useful thing he says.
- **The omen is not the thing.** 「逆節萌生。天地未形，而先為之征，其事是以不成，雜受其刑。」 He refuses when the evidence looks *best* — the enemy has just executed its best minister, which is the clearest possible sign. The visible sign is a 征, a symptom; the form has not appeared. Promoted to #3.
- **Do not pre-set the method.** See review of #7. 「用人無藝，往從其所」 is the operative rule and it was cut.
- **The criterion for a good victory.** 「是故戰勝而不報，取地而不反，兵勝於外，福生於內，用力甚少而名聲章明」 — win so there is nothing to come back for, take ground without needing to return it, win outside so the inside grows, spend almost nothing and let the reputation announce itself. The top 10 has rules about *when* to fight and *how* to fight and none about what counts as a win. Promoted to #11.
- **Making yourself smaller than the other side's rhetoric.** 「余雖靦然而人面哉，吾猶禽獸也，又安知是諓諓者乎？」 — he answers 吴's envoy by denying he can understand language, so the envoy has nothing to report. Then: 「君王已委制於執事之人矣。子往矣，無使執事之人得罪於子」 — release the envoy unharmed so he is not punished for carrying the refusal. Then he does not re-consult the king at all: 「范蠡不報於王，擊鼓興師以隨使者」. A communication procedure, entirely absent. Promoted to #12.
- **「臣聞之」 as a stance.** Six times in 國語/卷21 he opens with 臣聞之, 臣聞古之善用兵者, 臣聞命矣. He never says "I think"; he says "I heard it." It is a genuine epistemic habit — he executes transmitted doctrine rather than claiming invention — and it is the mechanism behind his best quote about the economy (the doctrine is 計然's, not his). Left out of the 12 because it never changes a decision on its own; it is the frame, not the rule.
- **Send the hard job to whoever has known hardship.** 「彼非不愛其弟，顧有所不能忍者也…至如少弟者，生而見我富，乘堅驅良逐狡兔，豈知財所從來，故輕棄之」 (史記/卷41). The research filed it in Gaps as well-sourced but "not a war or money rule" — it is a selection rule, and the seat has one. Also from Gaps: 「無曠其眾，以為亂梯」, now at #2.
- **Independent sources the research did not check.** It said no independent source was consulted for 越絕書/吳越春秋. 吳越春秋·勾踐歸國外傳第八 (勾踐九年) gives a Fan Li strategy that is *not* in 國語 or 史記 and is not the wait-for-the-moment strategy: 「臣請按師整兵，待其壞敗，隨而襲之，兵不血刃，士不旋踵。臣願大王匿聲無見其動，以觀其靜」 — sit on the army until their structure fails, then take them without blood, and hide your own movements while you do. The same chapter has 「外郭築城而缺西北，示服事吳也，不敢壅塞，內以取吳，故缺西北，而吳不知也」 — build the capital with a deliberate gap facing the overlord, which reads as submission and is actually the hole you will expand through. Both `attributed`, from a ~2nd-century text whose chronology conflicts with 史記. Not promoted: too late to sit beside 國語 on equal footing, but worth knowing it exists.
- **The persona.** 史記三家註·正義 quoting 會稽典錄: 「范蠡字少伯…本是楚宛三戶人，**佯狂倜儻負俗**」, and 文種's report of him: 「范蠡本國狂人，生有此病」 — answered with 「吾聞士有賢俊之姿，必有佯狂之譏，內懷獨見之明，外有不知之毀」. Inner clarity, outer apparent madness. That is the register 國語's 「吾猶禽獸也」 is written in, so it corroborates #12 rather than standing alone.

3. **Kind:** decision rule
   **In plain English:** A visible sign that the enemy is decaying is not the decay. Rebellion and disorder only *sprout*; until Heaven and Earth have taken their actual form, acting on the sign is what makes the enterprise fail and is what brings the punishment on you.

8. **Kind:** algorithm

## Gaps

- **The design's own Fan Li quote is not his.** design.md line 29 gives 「時不至，不可強生；事不究，不可強成」 to 國語·越語下. It is in 國語/卷21, but **inside 韋昭's note on 節事者與地** — the wikitext reads 「節事者與地。{{*|與地，法地也。時不至不可彊生，事不究不可彊成之屬。}}」. Those are a third-century CE commentator's words, glossing 與地, and 范蠡 does not speak them. The quote-check confirmed the characters are present in the named source, which is exactly the limit of what it was written to establish. If that line goes into a seat, it must be labelled 韋昭, not 范蠡.
- **Verification method.** I re-fetched Wikisource 國語/卷21, 史記/卷041, 史記/卷129, 吳越春秋/勾踐歸國外傳, 史記三家註/卷041 and 越絕書/卷14 and tested every clause above against the source text with the glosses stripped and variant characters normalised. Every clause in the Top 12 matched its named source. Two strings I had to correct from the research's wording rather than from the source: 「君行令」 (史記) vs 「君行制」 (國語), and the 玄月 date. All of these are Wikisource re-editions with no critical apparatus — a Zhonghua 史記 remains the final check, as the research said.
- **ctext.org still blocked**, as in the original run. 史記正義 (which carries 會稽典錄) is available on Wikisource and is how I sourced the 佯狂 material; a Zhonghua edition of the 集解/索隱/正義 triple would be better.
- **越絕書: partially checked, and it is not neutral.** I read 卷十四 (越絕德序外傳記第十八) and found three things worth a line — 「若卑辭以地讓之」 (cede the land as well as the person, a variant of #9), 「范蠡內視若盲，反聽若聾，度天關，涉天機」, and 「貴微而賤獲」. The same chapter says 「范蠡恐懼，逃於五湖」, which flatly contradicts the deliberate, reasoned exit in 國語 §8 and 史記 — a reason to treat the text's 序 as late and unreliable rather than as evidence. **I could not read 越絕書 卷1–13 or 15**: the fetches were refused, so I cannot claim there is no other 范蠡 material there. Worth one more pass.
- **吳越春秋 was not usable as equal evidence.** The 兵不血刃 and 缺西北 passages are real and verified, but 吳越春秋 is ~2nd c. CE, its campaign chronology conflicts with 史記, and 韋昭 is citing it by a chapter name ("某則") rather than quoting it — so it is being used as corroboration of later readers, not as a text of the 春秋 period.
- **Two late, uncorroborated sources I deliberately did not use.** 無能子/范蠡說 (Tang) gives 「吾聞陰謀人者，其禍必復」 and a long exchange in which 文種 argues that removing a menace is not the same as sinning against Heaven — good, but a Tang fictional dialogue with no manuscript pedigree I could check. 春秋戰國異辭 (late Qing) carries a farewell speech not in 國語 or 史記 — 「臣聞君子俟時計，不數謀；死不被疑，內不自欺」 — which would be a genuinely new item if true, but the page returned a redirect and I could not verify the text. Both are named here so a later run can decide.
- **史記's son-in-Chu speech stays `attributed`.** It is built on the 莊生 anecdote and is 史記's record of what he said, not a transcript. I have kept it inside #10 rather than standing it alone, so nothing depends on it.
- **未收於十二 but real, in case the seat is re-scoped:** 「善治生者，能擇人而任時」 (史記/卷129 — choose the people, then use the timing); 「王乃遣工以良金寫范蠡之狀而朝禮之，環會稽三百里者以為范蠡地」 (國語 §8 — the state wrote his image in gold and made him a hereditary fief, which is the other half of #5 and complicates it); 「持盈者與天…節事者與地」的 史記 variant 以地 for 與地, still unreconciled.
