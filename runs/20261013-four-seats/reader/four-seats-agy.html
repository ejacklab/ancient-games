Gaps

  • Attribution boundary of economic doctrine: The famous commercial rules in 史記·卷129 (「旱則資舟，水則資車」, 「貴上極則反賤，賤下極則反貴」, 「財幣欲其行如流水」) are spoken by Fan Li's teacher Ji Ran (計然), not by Fan Li. The biographical narrative descriptions of Fan Li's trading at Tao (「與時逐而不責於人。故善治生者，能擇人而任時」, 「十九年之中三致千金，再分散與貧交疏昆弟」) represent Sima Qian's narrative voice, not Fan Li's spoken decision rules. Fan Li's authentic spoken rule regarding commercial practice is strictly his decision to transfer Ji Ran's five proven state policies to his private household (#16).
  • Proverbs excluded: The proverb 「千金之子，不死於市」 cited in 史記·卷041 and 史記·卷129 is explicitly introduced as a popular saying (「吾聞千金之子不死於市」 / 「諺曰」), not Fan Li's own coined rule.
  • External and late texts uncheckable: Texts such as 越絕書, 吳越春秋, 會稽典錄 (cited in 史記正義), 無能子, and 春秋戰國異辭 were referenced in secondary commentaries but are absent from the local repository (runs/20261013-four-seats/sources/fan-li/). In accordance with the brief's instruction, no quotes from these unverified witnesses were promoted to the final list.
  • Wikitext commentary reconciliation: In 國語·卷21 §1, the phrase 「時不至，不可彊生；事不究，不可彊成」 appears in 韋昭's inline commentary on line 3, but also appears verbatim in Fan Li's main spoken speech on line 9; it is retained as authentic spoken text based on line 9.

Gaps

  • 〈吳問〉 and bamboo slip variants: The excavated Yinqueshan (銀雀山) bamboo slips include lost chapters such as 〈吳問〉 where Sun Tzu converses with King Helü. However, authoritative line-by-line collated readings could not be directly verified against primary tomb slips in this environment without risking unverified conjecture. Consequently, no items were manufactured from bamboo-only fragments.
  • The 《答話》 (Questions and Answers) appendix: Found in the Song edition received text of 《孫子兵法》 following Chapter 13. As noted by Song editors, these dialogues contain external attributions and potential interpolations (e.g. from 《孫子兵法論》 or later commentators). To preserve strict quote fidelity, they have been excluded from the core list of Sun Tzu's own mindsets and rules.
  • Biographical anecdotes in 《史記》: Sima Qian's famous biography (drilling the palace concubines) reflects narrative historiography written several centuries later rather than Sun Tzu's direct strategic and tactical rules, and was therefore omitted from the decision rules.
  • Excluded overly modern interpretations: Paraphrases claiming that "character is purely an output", "deception creates an uncopyable signature", or "siege ladder steps must be sequentially attempted" were excluded because the primary text does not state those modern systematic guarantees.

  1. Avoid big technical decisions where subordinates know the details better

    • Rule: A manager must avoid having to make big technical decisions between competing choices. If the people you manage come to you to make a technical decision, you are not competent to make it for them because they had better know the details better than you do.

  9. Open a two-week merge window for major changes, followed by stabilization release candidates

    • Kind: algorithm

  17. Write comments to explain what and why code works, never how

    • Kind: decision rule
    • Rule: Never attempt to explain how code works in a comment; write the code so that its operation is obvious. Use comments at the head of a function to tell what the code does and why it was written, avoiding redundant comments inside function bodies.

  18. Scale variable naming descriptiveness to scope: Spartan locals, descriptive globals

    • Kind: decision rule
    • Rule: Keep local variable names short and to the point (such as i or tmp), but mandate descriptive names for global variables and global functions. Encoding type information into names (Hungarian notation) is forbidden as unnecessary compiler clutter.
