# 孫子 (Sun Tzu) — where an insight belongs

## Answer

Not as stated. By where the record says the fix goes, 30 of the 56 insights sit in this repo's checks, tools or config (19 check, 9 tool, 2 config), and none of those is a requirement issue; by the lesson, my reading is 28 project / 22 agent-type / 6 task. A program can sort the written `Source:` quickly — your `insight index` already does — but it cannot sort the level reliably, because the level is not in the record; have the insight's author write it with two counterfactual answers and a `Stops if:` line, and the program's job becomes a read.

## How I decided

1. **Counted the record before judging (my #1).** `Source:` is the only level-like field the 56 sections all carry: 26 instruction, 19 check, 9 tool, 2 config. 「多算勝，少算不勝，而況於無算乎？」 (my #1, quoted). 2. **Named the ground (my #19; my reading).** Task level: the lesson is tied to this one task's own brief, spec, criterion or command, so a different task in this repo would not meet it and its home is the run folder. Project level: any agent working in this repo could meet it, and the cause is in this repo's code, tool, test or config. Agent-type: the cause is a habit or limit of an agent kind and follows it into any repo. The question defines only the last two; "task" is your third word, and it is the one I had to define myself.

9. **Put the work on the record's side (my #2, my #12, my #16).** 「不可勝在己，可勝在敵」 (my #2, quoted): the sorter cannot make the text classifiable; the writer can make the record carry the level. 「故善戰者，求之於勢，不責於人」 (my #12, quoted): change the ground, not the people. **Proposed change to the pattern** — R12.3's insight section gains two lines, written by the insight's author: `Level: task | project | agent-type` and `Stops if: <the artifact or habit that must change>`. It changes the insight artifact and the check — not the file's home or the run's shape.

Named ground, prescribed action (my #19): task stays in the run folder; project and agent-type stay there too and are found through the index — the index is the filing cabinet, not a new folder tree.

## What I need to know

- **Does "task level" mean one task, or a kind of task?** If you mean a kind (e.g. "writing a spec"), that is a fourth level, and my six task labels mostly move into it; the answer changes from "6 task" to "almost none".
- **Does "project requirement issues" mean gaps in SPEC/HARNESS_REQUIREMENTS only, or every defect in this repo's code, tools and tests?** The first leaves the eight breakers above outside the exception; the second puts them inside it and makes "project level" mean "anything in this repo", which empties the word.
- **One label per insight, or one per lesson?** If per lesson, F-8392b4ce (project fix) and F-0f2fa841 (agent-type fix) can each carry two, and the field belongs to the lesson, not the section.
- **Who fills the level — the writer as a claim, with your override in `insight-decisions.md`, or you as a decision?** I recommend the writer, because the evidence is in the whys; but R12.3 makes the fix your choice, and the level is not a fix.

## Costs and risks

- **A label is a claim, not a fact.** A writer in a hurry can file a project bug as agent-type to look general, or an agent habit as project to avoid a global rule; the lesson then goes to the wrong place and the noise you want to remove stays. Price: one more thing to get wrong per insight. Check: `Stops if:` names the artifact or habit, so a wrong level is visible (my #1: an uncounted claim is 無算; my #16: clear orders before enforcement).
- **Backfilling 56 is a migration.** It costs time and breaks links, and the run folder is where the evidence lives (R12.3). Price: the old 56 carry no level until loaded. My #5 (move only on advantage) and my #3 (whole over broken): do not move them.
- **A guessed sorter will be wrong on the mixed 13.** Price: false confidence, and the wrong lesson loaded. My #10 (no constant form) and my #11: do not build it; if built, let it validate a written value, never assign one.
- **The project bucket can become the dump.** If every guard/tool/test defect is project-level, the bucket grows and "reduce noise" fails. Price: your stated benefit. My #6 (mix benefit and harm): the benefit is completeness, the harm is noise. Keep `Source` as a second axis, so a reader can ask for "project-level testing" rather than all project.
- **An agent-type rule can be wrong on a new repo.** A rule that follows the agent may meet ground where it does not hold (a sandbox behaviour, a quota). My #4 (know the ground): name the condition in the insight, not just the rule.

## How we would know I was right

1. **Agreement test — is the level one thing?** Take 20 sections (a mix of my 28/22/6) and give them to two writers who see neither my labels nor each other's. Each fills `Level:` from the two questions. At least 16 of 20 agreeing means the level is stable enough to record; 14 or fewer means the three levels are not one thing and the cut is wrong. If they disagree exactly on the mixed sections I named, the field needs a tie-break: the proximate artifact in `Stops if:`.
2. **Learning test — is it filed where it is learned from?** After 10 new insights, count how often a level-filtered `insight show` is loaded at the start of a task and cited in the plan. 0 of 10 means the level is decoration; 3 or more with a changed plan means it is doing work. The guard can count the loads; the transcript shows the citation. This is a count, not a judgment (my #1).
3. **Sorter test — read, not guess.** The program's `level` must equal the writer's for 56 of 56. A single disagreement means it is inferring, and it should be removed.
