## Final list

### 1. Keep to the time: Heaven's time and human affairs together
- **Kind:** decision rule
- **Rule:** While Heaven's time has not arisen, do not be the one who attacks; while human affairs have not arisen,
  do not be the one who starts. Success needs human affairs joined with Heaven and Earth. When one had come and the
  other had not, he told the king to wait.
- **Their words:** 「夫聖人隨時以行，是謂守時。天時不作，弗為人客；人事不起，弗為之始。」 — "The sage moves with the
  time; this is called keeping to the time. When Heaven's time has not arisen, do not be the guest [the attacker];
  when human affairs have not arisen, do not be the one who begins." / 「人事至矣，天應未也，王姑待之。」 — "Human
  affairs have arrived; Heaven's response has not. For now, wait." / 「天應至矣，人事未盡也，王姑待之。」…
  「夫人事必將與天地相參，然後乃可以成功。」 — "Heaven's response has arrived; human affairs are not yet at their
  end. For now, wait." … "Human affairs must be joined with Heaven and Earth as a three; only then can success be
  achieved." (韋昭: 攻者為客; 先動為始; 人事 = 怨叛、逆亂之萌; 參，三也.)
- **Source:** 國語·越語下 (Wikisource 國語/卷21): §范蠡進諫句踐持盈定傾節事 (國語21:3). There he tells the king that
  attacking now 「逆於天而不和於人」; the king attacked and lost (21:5). Also §范蠡謂人事至而天應未至 (21:15), on Wu's
  internal decay, and §范蠡謂人事與天地相參乃可以成功 (21:19).
- **Words are:** quoted
- **From:** top-12 #1 (split), with top-10 #1's quotes restored

### 2. Three kinds of state affairs, three things to go by
- **Kind:** decision rule
- **Rule:** A state's affairs are keeping what is full, steadying what is tilting, and regulating affairs. Keeping
  the full goes with Heaven, steadying the tilting goes with people, and regulating affairs goes with Earth. Heaven's
  way, in his words, is full without overflowing, flourishing without arrogance, and labouring without boasting of
  its work.
- **Their words:** 「夫國家之事，有持盈，有定傾，有節事。」/「持盈者與天，定傾者與人，節事者與地。」/
  「天道盈而不溢，盛而不驕，勞而不矜其功。」 — "In the affairs of a state there is keeping the full, steadying the
  tilting, and regulating affairs." / "Keeping the full goes with Heaven; steadying the tilting goes with people;
  regulating affairs goes with Earth." / "Heaven's way is full but does not overflow, flourishes but is not arrogant,
  labours but does not boast of its work." (韋昭: 持，守也; 盈，滿也; 傾，危也; 節，制也; 與天，法天也.)
- **Source:** 國語21:3. He repeats it after the defeat at 21:7 (「君王其忘之乎？持盈者與天…」) and fills in two of the
  three: 與人 at 21:7 (→ #15) and 與地 at 21:9 (→ #3). 史記·越王句踐世家 41:18 has the variant
  「持滿者與天，定傾者與人，節事者以地」.
- **Words are:** quoted
- **From:** top-12 #1 (split)

### 3. While the time has not come
- **Kind:** decision rule
- **Rule:** When the time has not come, growth cannot be forced, and when a matter has not run its course, completion
  cannot be forced. Stay as you are, take the measure of the world, wait for what comes and set it right, and keep
  the people working, fed and not idle. When the time turns and an opening appears, one must know the constant
  measures of Heaven and Earth; if there is no opening and no turn, care for and protect the people and wait.
- **Their words:** 「時不至，不可彊生；事不究，不可彊成。自若以處，以度天下，待其來者而正之，因時之所宜而定之。
  同男女之功，除民之害，以避天殃。田野開闢，府倉實，民眾殷。無曠其眾，以為亂梯。時將有反，事將有閒，
  必有以知天地之恆制，乃可以有天下之成利。事無閒，時無反，則撫民保教以須之。」 — "When the time has not come,
  growth cannot be forced; when the matter has not run its course, completion cannot be forced. Stay as you are and
  take the measure of the world; wait for what comes and set it right; settle it by what the time makes fitting. Join
  the work of men and women, remove the people's harms, and so avoid Heaven's punishment. Fields opened, storehouses
  full, people numerous. Do not leave the people idle — that becomes a ladder to disorder. When the time is about to
  turn and affairs are about to show an opening, one must have the means to know the constant measures of Heaven and
  Earth; then one can have the world's full benefit. If affairs show no opening and the time does not turn, care for
  the people, protect and teach them, and wait." (韋昭: 自如，無妄動也; 待其來 = 不先唱; 究，窮也.)
- **Source:** 國語21:9, his answer to 「節事奈何？」 after returning from Wu.
- **Words are:** quoted
- **From:** top-12 #2

### 4. When the time comes, go; having it, finish
- **Kind:** decision rule
- **Rule:** Follow the time the way you fight a fire or chase a runaway: hurry, afraid only of arriving late. Having
  got the time, do not slacken. What Heaven gives and is not taken, or a time that is got but not carried through,
  turns back on you as calamity.
- **Their words:** 「微君王之言，臣故將謁之。臣聞從時者，猶救火、追亡人也，蹶而趨之，唯恐弗及。」 — "Had you not
  said it, I was about to ask for it. I have heard: one who follows the time is like one fighting a fire or chasing a
  runaway — run and hurry, fearing only not to get there." / 「得時無怠，時不再來，天予不取，反為之災。」 — "Having got
  the time, do not slacken; the time does not come again; what Heaven gives and you do not take turns into
  disaster." / 「得時不成，天有還形。天節不遠，五年復反，小凶則近，大凶則遠。」 — "If you get the time and do not
  complete it, Heaven has a way of turning it back. Heaven's period is not far: in five years it comes round — near
  for a small misfortune, far for a great one." (韋昭: 小凶 = 危敗, 大凶 = 死滅; near = five years, far = ten or
  twenty.) / 史記: 「天與弗取，反受其咎。」 — "If Heaven gives and you do not take, you receive the blame in return."
- **Source:** 國語21:21 (玄月, the ninth month per 韋昭; the king says the year is late, and the army marches). 21:23:
  said while advising *against* accepting Wu's five challenges in one day. 21:28: said against accepting Wu's first
  peace mission. The same 「得時不成，反受其殃」 appears at 21:13. 史記41:32: said against accepting Wu's last peace
  mission. Finish: after the king handed him the reply (21:30), 「范蠡不報於王，擊鼓興師以隨使者…遂滅吳」 (21:32,
  narration).
- **Words are:** quoted (the march at 21:32 is attributed — narration)
- **From:** top-12 #4

### 5. The plan does not shift
- **Kind:** decision rule
- **Rule:** A plan made at court should not be lost on the battlefield. Advance and retreat turn into each other and
  Heaven's rhythm changes; only the plan does not shift. Years of planning are not to be thrown away in a morning.
- **Their words:** 「夫謀之廊廟，失之中原，其可乎？王姑勿許也。…贏縮轉化，後將悔之。天節固然，唯謀不遷。」 — "To
  plan it in the temple hall and lose it on the central plain — can that be right? For now, do not accept. …
  Advance and retreat change into each other; later you would regret it. Heaven's rhythm is like that; only the plan
  does not move." (韋昭: 贏縮，進退也; 固然，有轉化也; 謀必素定，不可遷易.) /
  「夫十年謀之，一朝而棄之，其可乎？王姑勿許，其事將易冀已。」 — "Ten years of planning, thrown away in one morning —
  can that be right? For now, do not accept; the matter will then be easy to hope for." / 史記:
  「謀之二十二年，一旦而棄之，可乎？」
- **Source:** 國語21:23 (Wu challenged to battle five times in a day and the king wanted to accept); 21:30 (Wu's envoy
  came back humbler and the king wanted to accept peace); 史記41:32. A later witness: 越絕書·請糴內傳 (卷五)
  「君王圖之廊廟，失之中野，可乎？謀之七年，須臾棄之。」
- **Words are:** quoted
- **From:** top-12 #6

### 6. Leaving
- **Kind:** mindset
- **Rule:** A servant labours when his ruler is anxious and dies when his ruler is disgraced. He had stayed alive after
  會稽 only for this task, so when it was done he asked for the 會稽 punishment and refused the share of the state.
  He warned 文種 that when the birds are gone the bow is put away, and that Goujian could share hardship but not joy.
  At the peak in Qi he said that holding an honoured name for long is unlucky.
- **Their words:** 「臣聞之，為人臣者，君憂臣勞，君辱臣死。昔者君王辱於會稽，臣所以不死者，為此事也。今事已濟矣，
  蠡請從會稽之罰。」…「臣聞命矣。君行制，臣行意。」 — "I have heard: a servant labours when his ruler is anxious,
  and dies when his ruler is disgraced. When you were disgraced at 會稽, the reason I did not die was this task.
  Now the task is done; I ask to receive the punishment of 會稽." … [to the threat: share the state, or die with
  wife and children] "I have heard your command. You act by your law; I act by my intent." (韋昭: 制，法也;
  意，志也. 史記41:56 reads 君行令.) / 「蜚鳥盡，良弓藏；狡兔死，走狗烹。越王爲人長頸鳥喙，可與共患難，不可與共樂。
  子何不去？」 — "When the flying birds are gone, the good bow is put away; when the cunning hare is dead, the
  hunting dog is cooked. The King of Yue has a long neck and a bird's beak; one can share hardship with him, not joy.
  Why don't you leave?" / 「居家則致千金，居官則至卿相，此布衣之極也。久受尊名，不祥。」 — "At home I have reached a
  thousand in gold; in office, chief minister — the limit for a man in plain cloth. To hold an honoured name long is
  unlucky."
- **Source:** 國語21:34 (§范蠡乘輕舟以浮於五湖); 史記41:56 (the same duty line in his letter to Goujian); 史記41:36
  (his letter to 文種, from Qi); 史記41:58 (Qi, followed by the narration 「乃歸相印，盡散其財」).
- **Words are:** quoted. The narrator's reasons at 史記41:56 (「范蠡以爲大名之下，難以久居，且句踐爲人可與同患，難與處安」)
  and the departures are attributed.
- **From:** top-12 #5

### 7. Do not plan early
- **Kind:** decision rule
- **Rule:** While the Lord on High has not brought it to completion, wait for the time to turn; seeking it by force
  is unlucky. There is taking back, giving and not giving, so do not plan early. Wu is already the king's, and
  planning for it early would make the outcome unknowable again.
- **Their words:** 「未可也。蠡聞之，上帝不考，時反是守，彊索者不祥。得時不成，反受其殃。失德滅名，流走死亡。有奪，有予，
  有不予，王無蚤圖。夫吳，君王之吳也，王若蚤圖之，其事又將未可知也。」 — "Not yet. I have heard: when the Lord on
  High has not completed it, keep watch for the time to turn; one who seeks by force is unlucky. Get the time and
  not complete it, and you receive its calamity; lose virtue and your name is destroyed, you flee and die. There is
  taking back, there is giving, there is not giving. Do not plan early. Wu is Your Majesty's Wu; if you plan for it
  early, the matter will again be unknowable." (韋昭: 考，成也; 天時反，乃可以動; 索，求也.)
- **Source:** 國語21:13 (§范蠡勸句踐無蚤圖吳), the first of four yearly "not yet" answers.
- **Words are:** quoted
- **From:** new (my research)

### 8. A fresh disaster can unite the enemy
- **Kind:** decision rule
- **Rule:** When the enemy's disaster is new, its people are afraid, and everyone from the ruler down knows their
  resources cannot last, they will pool their strength and fight to the death, so attacking is still dangerous.
  Meanwhile hunt, feast and drink without excess and without forgetting the state's standing rules. Their ruler will
  thin his virtue, their people will use up their strength and they will look for food and not get it; then
  Heaven's and Earth's punishment can be brought on. Until then, wait.
- **Their words:** 「今其禍新民恐，其君臣上下，皆知其資財之不足以支長久也，彼將同其力，致其死，猶尚殆。王其且馳騁弋獵，
  無至禽荒；宮中之樂，無至酒荒；肆與大夫觴飲，無忘國常。彼其上將薄其德，民將盡其力，又使之望而不得食，
  乃可以致天地之殛。王姑待之。」 — "Now their disaster is new and the people are afraid; ruler and ministers, high
  and low, all know their wealth will not hold out long. They will unite their strength and give their lives — it is
  still dangerous. Your Majesty, go riding and hunting for now, but not to the point of being lost in the hunt;
  palace pleasures, but not to drunken ruin; drink freely with the officers, but do not forget the state's standing
  rules. Their ruler will thin his virtue, their people will exhaust their strength, and they will look for food and
  not get it; then the punishment of Heaven and Earth can be brought about. For now, wait." (Why hunt: 韋昭
  「示不以吳為念」 — to show Yue is not thinking of Wu. That purpose is the commentator's, not Fan Li's words.)
- **Source:** 國語21:19. He said it after crabs had eaten Wu's rice (「稻蟹不遺種」) and the king angrily asked why he
  kept switching between 人事 and 天時. 韋昭: Yue attacked four years later.
- **Words are:** quoted
- **From:** new (codex "Missing" #1, checked by me against 國語21:19)

### 9. Disorder that has only sprouted
- **Kind:** decision rule
- **Rule:** When Wu killed its remonstrating minister, he said that rebellion against order had only sprouted and
  Heaven and Earth had not yet shown their form. To campaign ahead of that is why a venture fails, and everyone
  shares the harm, so wait.
- **Their words:** 「逆節萌生。天地未形，而先為之征，其事是以不成，雜受其刑。王姑待之。」 — "Rebellion against order
  is sprouting. Heaven and Earth have not yet taken visible form, and to campaign ahead of it — that is why the
  affair does not succeed, and all together suffer the harm. For now, wait." (韋昭: 萌，兆也; 形，見也，天地之占未見;
  征，征伐也; 雜，猶俱也; 刑，害也.)
- **Source:** 國語21:17 (§范蠡謂先為之征其事不成), on news that 申胥 (伍子胥) had been killed. 史記41:28 records the same
  case briefly: 「呉已殺子胥，導諛者衆，可乎？」對曰：「未可。」 At 41:30 he says 「可矣」 the next spring. In the
  narrator's words, Wu's crack troops had then gone north with the king, leaving only the old, the weak and the
  heir.
- **Words are:** quoted (the conditions at 史記41:30 are attributed — narration)
- **From:** top-12 #3

### 10. Turning at the extreme, and stopping
- **Kind:** mindset
- **Rule:** Those of old who used troops well took advance and retreat as the norm and the four seasons as the
  measure. They did not go past Heaven's limit, and they stopped when the count ran out. Yang at its height turns to
  yin and yin to yang; the sun at its end comes back, and the full moon begins to wane.
- **Their words:** 「臣聞古之善用兵者，贏縮以為常，四時以為紀，無過天極，究數而止。天道皇皇，日月以為常，明者以為法，
  微者則是行。陽至而陰，陰至而陽；日困而還，月盈而匡。古之善用兵者，因天地之常，與之俱行。」 — "I have heard that
  those of old who used troops well took advance and retreat as their constant and the four seasons as their rule;
  they did not pass Heaven's limit, and stopped when the count was exhausted. Heaven's way is bright, with sun and
  moon as its constant; the bright is taken as the model, the waning is acted on accordingly. When yang reaches its
  height it turns to yin; when yin reaches its height, to yang. The sun, exhausted, returns; the moon, full, wanes.
  Those of old who used troops well went by the constants of Heaven and Earth and moved with them." (韋昭 on
  究數而止: 「無過天道之所至，窮其數而止也」; on 明/微: advance when sun and moon are full, withdraw when they wane.)
- **Source:** 國語21:25, the opening of his 「臣聞古之善用兵者」 speech during the campaign.
- **Words are:** quoted (he gives it as received teaching — 臣聞)
- **From:** new (my research; split out of the passage behind top-12 #8)

### 11. People start it, Heaven and Earth show it, the sage completes it
- **Kind:** mindset
- **Rule:** With enemy states and decisions taken on the spot, go by the constants of yin and yang and the norms of
  Heaven and Earth: be soft without bending and strong without hardness. Heaven goes by people and the sage goes by
  Heaven: people bring a thing about, Heaven and Earth make it visible, and the sage completes it. So a victory is
  not repaid, land taken is not given back, and very little force is used.
- **Their words:** 「四封之外，敵國之制，立斷之事，因陰陽之恆，順天地之常，柔而不屈，彊而不剛，德虐之行，因以為常；
  死生因天地之刑，天因人，聖人因天；人自生之，天地形之，聖人因而成之。是故戰勝而不報，取地而不反，兵勝於外，福生於內，
  用力甚少而名聲章明，種亦不如蠡也。」 — "Outside the four borders — dealing with enemy states, matters decided on the
  spot: go by the constant of yin and yang, follow the norm of Heaven and Earth; be soft but not bent, strong but
  not hard; let favour and severity follow these as the rule; let death and life follow the law of Heaven and Earth.
  Heaven goes by people; the sage goes by Heaven. People bring it about themselves, Heaven and Earth give it form,
  the sage goes by that and completes it. So battles are won and not repaid, land is taken and not given back, arms
  win outside and good fortune grows inside, very little force is used and the name shines — in this 種 is not as
  good as I." (韋昭: 德 = 懷柔及爵賞, 虐 = 斬伐及黜奪; 戰勝而不報 = 敵家不能報也.)
- **Source:** 國語21:11, his reply when the king said 「不穀之國家，蠡之國家也，蠡其圖之！」. The king then had 種
  govern the state (「令大夫種為之」; 韋昭: 為，治國也).
- **Words are:** quoted
- **From:** top-12 #11 (merged, widened to the whole passage)

### 12. When to engage
- **Kind:** decision rule
- **Rule:** If they come at you, hold firm and do not engage. Engage only on a disaster from Heaven and Earth,
  cross-checked by whether their people are hungry or fed and worn or rested; let their yang run out and your yin
  fill, then take it. A side that is hard, strong and swift cannot be taken while its yang has not run out. Seeing
  Wu come that way, he said wait.
- **Their words:** 「剛彊以禦，陽節不盡，不死其野。彼來從我，固守勿與。若將與之，必因天地之災，
  又觀其民之饑飽勞逸以參之。盡其陽節、盈吾陰節而奪之。宜為人客，剛彊而力疾；陽節不盡，輕而不可取。宜為人主，安徐而重固；
  陰節不盡，柔而不可迫。」…「今其來也，剛彊而力疾，王姑待之。」 — "If they resist with hardness and strength, their
  yang phase is not exhausted; they will not die in the field. If they come at us, hold firm and do not engage. If
  we are to engage them, it must be by a disaster of Heaven and Earth, and we also look at their people's hunger or
  fullness, toil or ease to cross-check. Exhaust their yang phase, fill our yin phase, and take it. A side fit to be
  the guest [attacker] is hard, strong and swift; while its yang phase is not exhausted, though it seems careless it
  cannot be taken. A side fit to be the host is calm, slow, heavy and firm; while its yin phase is not exhausted,
  though soft it cannot be pressed." … "Now they come hard, strong and swift. For now, wait." (The subject of
  宜為人客 / 宜為人主 is read as 韋昭 reads it.)
- **Source:** 國語21:25 (§越興師伐吳而弗與戰). Outcome at 21:28: 「居軍三年，吳師自潰」 (narration).
- **Words are:** quoted
- **From:** top-12 #8

### 13. Contention is the last of affairs
- **Kind:** decision rule
- **Rule:** Courage goes against virtue, weapons are instruments of ill omen, and contention is the last of affairs.
  Whoever starts on others is finished by them, and whoever does these things first does not profit.
- **Their words:** 「夫勇者，逆德也；兵者，凶器也；爭者，事之末也。陰謀逆德，好用凶器，始於人者，人之所卒也；
  淫佚之事，上帝之禁也，先行此者，不利。」 — "Courage goes against virtue; weapons are ill-omened instruments;
  contention is the last of affairs. Secret scheming goes against virtue; fondness for ill-omened instruments — who
  starts on others is ended by them; dissipation is what the Lord on High forbids. Whoever does these first does not
  profit." (韋昭: 勇則攻奪; 陰謀，兵謀也; 始以伐人，人終害之.) / 史記:
  「臣聞兵者凶器也，戰者逆德也，爭者事之末也。陰謀逆德，好用凶器，試身於所末，上帝禁之，行者不利。」
- **Source:** 國語21:5, said when the king was about to attack Wu first. The king answered 「吾已斷之矣」, attacked, and
  was defeated at 五湖 (narration). Also 史記41:16.
- **Words are:** quoted
- **From:** top-12 #7

### 14. Say who is better at what
- **Kind:** decision rule
- **Rule:** Asked to stay behind and keep the state, he said he was not as good as 種 at the people's affairs inside
  the borders, and that 種 was not as good as he at dealing with enemy states and deciding on the spot. The king left
  種 to keep the state and took Fan Li to Wu.
- **Their words:** 「四封之內，百姓之事，蠡不如種也。四封之外，敵國之制，立斷之事，種亦不如蠡也。」 — "Inside the four
  borders, the people's affairs: I am not as good as 種. Outside the four borders, dealing with enemy states, matters
  decided on the spot: 種 is not as good as I." / 史記: 「兵甲之事，種不如蠡；塡撫國家，親附百姓，蠡不如種。」 — "In
  arms and armour, 種 is not as good as I; in settling the state and winning the people, I am not as good as 種."
- **Source:** 國語21:7, in answer to 「蠡為我守於國」, followed by 「令大夫種守於國，與范蠡入宦於吳」 (narration); again at
  21:11. 史記41:22, followed by 「舉國政屬大夫種」.
- **Words are:** quoted
- **From:** top-12 #10 (split)

### 15. Steadying what is tilting: humble yourself, then bargain with your person
- **Kind:** decision rule
- **Rule:** Steadying what is tilting goes with people: humble words, honoured ritual, treasures and women musicians,
  and a title of honour for the other side. If that does not end it, bargain with your own person.
- **Their words:** 「卑辭尊禮，玩好女樂，尊之以名。如此不已，又身與之市。」 — "Humble words and honoured ritual;
  treasures and women musicians; honour him with a name. If that does not end it, then bargain with your own
  person." (韋昭: 「市，利也，謂委管籥屬國家，以身隨之」 — hand over the keys, put the state under them, and follow in
  person.) / 史記: 「卑辭厚禮以遺之，不許，而身與之市。」
- **Source:** 國語21:7, after the defeat, besieged at 會稽. 種's offer that follows: 「請委管籥屬國家，以身隨之，君王制之」.
  Also 史記41:18.
- **Words are:** quoted
- **From:** top-12 #9

### 16. Take a method that worked for the state to the household
- **Kind:** decision rule
- **Rule:** After the revenge he said that 計然's policies numbered seven, that Yue used five and got what it wanted,
  and that having applied them to the state he wanted to use them for his household.
- **Their words:** 「計然之策七，越用其五而得意。既已施於國，吾欲用之家。」 — "Ji Ran's policies were seven; Yue used
  five of them and got what it wanted. Since they have been applied to the state, I want to use them for the
  household." (漢書·貨殖傳 reads 「計然之策，十用其五」.)
- **Source:** 史記·貨殖列傳 (史記129:24). What follows is the narrator's: at Tao 「與時逐而不責於人」 and
  「十九年之中三致千金，再分散與貧交疏昆弟」.
- **Words are:** quoted (the Tao account is attributed)
- **From:** new (codex "Missing" #2, checked by me against 史記129:24)

### 17. Send the one who can part with money
- **Kind:** decision rule
- **Rule:** He wanted to send his youngest son with the gold, because the youngest, born into wealth, could part with
  money, while the eldest, who had known hardship, could not. When the eldest went instead and his brother was
  executed, Fan Li called it the logic of things.
- **Their words:** 「彼非不愛其弟，顧有所不能忍者也。是少與我倶，見苦，爲生難，故重棄財。至如少弟者，生而見我富，
  乘堅驅良逐狡兔，豈知財所從來，故輕棄之，非所惜吝。前日吾所爲欲遣少子，固爲其能棄財故也。而長者不能，故卒以殺其弟，
  事之理也，無足悲者。」 — "It is not that he did not love his brother; there was something he could not bear. He was
  with me when young, saw hardship, knew how hard a living is, so he finds it hard to let money go. The youngest was
  born to see me rich, riding sound carriages behind good horses chasing hares — how would he know where wealth
  comes from? So he lets it go lightly and does not begrudge it. The reason I wanted to send the youngest was
  precisely that he could let money go. The eldest could not, and so in the end he got his brother killed. That is
  the logic of things; nothing to grieve over."
- **Source:** 史記41:60–70 (the 莊生 episode in Chu). At 41:60 the narrator says the mother and the eldest son pressed
  him into sending the eldest (「不得已而遣長子」).
- **Words are:** quoted (direct speech in 史記, inside an anecdote; the surrounding events are attributed)
- **From:** top-12 #10 (split; reading corrected)

### 18. Examine the preparations
- **Kind:** decision rule
- **Rule:** Goujian asked his officers what would make it possible to fight Wu. Fan Li answered with a question:
  with the preparations examined, can we fight? The king called it "skilful".
- **Their words:** 「審備則可以戰乎？」王曰：「巧。」 — "If the preparations are examined, can we then fight?" The king:
  "Skilful." (韋昭: 備，守禦之備; 巧，審密不可攻入也 — defensive preparations, so close-knit they cannot be broken
  into.)
- **Source:** 國語·吳語 (Wikisource 國語/卷19), §句踐滅吳夫差自殺 — the five officers' answers: 舌庸 審賞, 苦成 審罰, 種
  審物, 蠡 審備, 皋如 審聲.
- **Words are:** quoted
- **From:** new (my research)

## Gaps

- **What "quoted" means here.** It means direct speech given to him in 國語 or 史記. None of it is his own writing.
  漢書·藝文志 (兵書略·兵權謀) lists a 《范蠡》 in two 篇, which is lost. Narration in those two books is marked
  attributed. Speech given to him in the Eastern Han books 越絕書 and 吳越春秋 would also be attributed. None of it is
  used as a main quote in the list.
- **Whose words are 越語下's?** 白奚 (2020) writes that early studies of the Mawangdui 《黃帝四經》 found that 「很多文句同
  《国语·越语下》中的范蠡之言有很高的相似度和相关度」, and he argues Fan Li was a precursor of 黃老
  (https://www.nopss.gov.cn/n1/2020/0929/c430755-31879723.html; sentence checked in the page HTML). This leaves open
  whether 越語下 records his thought or puts a school's doctrine in his mouth. I did not settle it: *not measured*.
- **Left out because it cannot be stated without choosing.** 「用人無藝，往從其所」 (國語21:25): 韋昭 reads it as "no fixed
  rule — go by the enemy" (因敵為制，不豫設也). 經義述聞 joins 用人無藝 to the line before it and 往從其所 to the line
  after it. Also left out: the formation lines (設右以為牝，益左以為牡…), whose meaning I could not state plainly.
- **One reading chosen.** For 宜為人客… / 宜為人主… (#12) I followed 韋昭. Another reading takes them as instructions to
  your own side, and the grammar allows either.
- **A conflicting witness.** 越絕書·吳內傳 (卷三) has the three affairs with a different pairing: 「天貴持盈…地貴定傾…
  人貴節事」. It also sets the speech against 闔廬 instead of 夫差. I followed 國語 and 史記, which agree with each
  other.
- **The 計然 boundary.** 史記 gives 「知斗則修備…財幣欲其行如流水」 and 「旱則資舟，水則資車」 to 計然, so they are not on
  the list. Who 計然 was is disputed. 容齋隨筆, as quoted in 文獻通考 卷213, gives 蔡謨: 「計然者范蠡所著書篇名耳非人也」.
  The same passage gives 顏師古's rejection of that view, and 徐廣 calls 計然 范蠡's teacher. If 蔡謨 is right, the
  commerce doctrine would be Fan Li's. I could not decide this from the texts.
- **Two accounts of the "go" decision.** 國語21:21 has 玄月, when the king says the year is late. 史記41:30 has the next
  spring, when Wu's army had gone to 黃池; after that raid, Yue 「自度亦未能滅呉，乃與呉平」. I cite each in its own
  words and did not reconcile them.
- **Real, but only in late texts (Eastern Han), so not on the list.** I fetched and read them on 2026-10-13. None is
  in 國語 or 史記, and 越絕書 卷十四 itself says 「范蠡恐懼，逃於五湖」, which contradicts their reasoned exit.
  - 「臣聞君子俟時，計不數謀，死不被疑，內不自欺」 — 吳越春秋·勾踐伐吳外傳. The top 12 could not verify this line from
    春秋戰國異辭; it is here.
  - 「知數不用，知懼不去，豈謂智與？」 — 越絕書 卷六, his judgement of 伍子胥 staying.
  - 「臣聞亡國之臣，不敢語政，敗軍之將，不敢語勇」 — 吳越春秋·勾踐入臣外傳.
  - 「臣聞：『謀國破敵，動觀其符。』」 and the 「按師整兵，待其壞敗，隨而襲之」 speech — 吳越春秋·勾踐歸國外傳.
  - 「審備慎守以待不虞，備設守固，必可應難」 — 吳越春秋·勾踐伐吳外傳, a later expansion of #18.
  - 「臣不敢殺主」 — 越絕書 卷五 and 卷十.
  - The whole 越絕書·枕中 (卷十三) 范子 dialogue: 「左道右術，去末取實」, 「名過實，則百姓不附親，賢士不為用」,
    「兵之要在於人，人之要在於穀…王而備此二者，然後可以圖之也」, plus 陰陽 grain-price forecasting.
  - The tasting-the-stool advice — 吳越春秋·勾踐入臣外傳.
