# 老子 (Laozi) — use cases must, requirements should: does it hold, and where it breaks

## Answer

It holds at step 2 (the user decides what is covered) and at step 3 (the result is judged against what the user asked for), and it breaks at step 4 — "requirements cover details the use cases leave unclear" makes the requirement a document instead of the one comparison that can fail a case, and R11.1's denominator is fixed from requirement text, so a work with use cases and no requirement has no count at all. What I would change: keep requirements SHOULD to exist and make the *comparison* — each accepted use case's "fails if" — the thing that has to exist, keep exactly one MUST and put it on the safety bound rather than on an artifact class, and split the AI's suggestion from the user's acceptance into two records with two makers and two dates.

## How I decided

1. **Read EJ's quote against the restatement before anything else.** My reading: the six steps are faithful, and the quote's real weight sits in one clause — "most important the results are valid for use cases". That clause is the direction's spine; everything else is scaffolding around it. My #17 is why the spine is right: 故有之以為利，無之以為用 — "what is there makes for benefit; what is not there makes for use" (`quoted`) — and the use of a use case is not the situation it describes, it is the empty space where a wrong result would be caught. The direction puts that emptiness in the optional artifact.

2. **Verdict, what holds: the user decides, and the decision is real.** My #10: 我有三寶，持而保之。一曰慈，二曰儉，三曰不敢為天下先 — "I have three treasures; I hold them. First compassion, second frugality, third not daring to be first in the world" (`quoted`). An AI that may only suggest, and a user who decides cover-or-not, is the agent refusing first place. The trial shows what is bought by that refusal: the 200-case brainstorm still found a clause with no "fails if", a "fails if" with no test and no code, and a case contradicting a settled decision — because my #8, 夫唯病病，是以不病 — "only by treating sickness as sickness is one not sick" (`quoted`), a contradiction that is surfaced is a sickness being treated, whether or not the user accepts the case. So step 2 earns its place in the direction.

3. **Verdict, what breaks: the denominator loses its source.** R11.1 fixes units from *the requirement's text and its fails-if*. EJ's direction makes the requirement SHOULD. Concrete case: a work that ships from use cases alone — EJ's own words make this legal ("use cases are must", requirements optional). No requirement, no units, no ratio. My #17 again: the emptiness is where the use is, and nothing fails; the report simply says nothing about coverage, which is the shape of the seven recorded harness failures (exit 0 with no work). My #14: 不自見故明 — "not displaying himself, he is clear" (`quoted`) — the opposite: not reporting anything, nothing is clear.

4. **Verdict, what breaks: "details that the use cases leave unclear" is the wrong job description.** Concrete case, from the trial: the requirement clause with no "fails if" (the rename clause, W1.1b) has no test and no code. A use case would never have surfaced it, because a case says what should happen and a "fails if" says what is wrong — and the trial already agreed that separation: evaluation rules state what makes a result wrong, not how to be right (R12.4). So the requirement is not the elaboration the use case leaves out; the requirement *is* the comparison. My #6: 其未兆易謀 … 為之於未有，治之於未亂 — "what has not yet shown a sign is easy to plan for; act on it before it exists; order it before it is in disorder" (`quoted`) — the case is the 未兆 state and cheap to hold; the comparison is what it can be held *against*.

5. **Change 1 — a requirement's role is "the comparison", and it stays SHOULD.** Each accepted use case names the one condition that makes it wrong, and that condition is the unit. Check: every accepted case maps to a unit whose fails-if is a comparison; **plant** — add a case with no fails-if and confirm the covered count does not rise. If it rises, the denominator moved and R11.1's rule 1 (units before cases) is already broken. Price: my #2, 禍莫大於不知足 … 故知足之足，常足矣 — "no calamity is greater than not knowing what is enough; so the enough of knowing enough is a lasting enough" (`quoted`) — how many conditions each case gets is EJ's to decide, not mine.

6. **Change 2 — the pair moves together, so "SHOULD" stays honest.** A case cannot be accepted without its comparison, and a comparison with no case attached stays out of scope until one does. Nothing is lost by this: the *document* is optional, the *comparison* is not. Check: the count of accepted cases equals the count of units that are covered or marked skippable; a mismatch is a fail, not a note. Price: the clause in R11.1 saying a generated case fitting no unit "is added as a new unit by the plan writer with a written reason, or recorded as out of scope" must also apply to a case the user wrote — that is my reading, and it is the clause this change adds.

7. **Change 3 — one MUST, on the safety bound.** My #14: 少則得，多則惑 — "little, then gained; much, then confused" (`quoted`). EJ's two musts ("use cases are must", "requirements are must follow once they exist") are both method rules, and the house rule gives a method rule SHOULD at most; the real MUST is already in R11.1 rule 6 — a safety unit is never skippable — and it is a property of a *unit*, not of an artifact class. Check: run the requirement-check on the rewritten sentence; if "use cases are must" survives as a method rule, it fails. Price, stated honestly: this removes the word that made EJ's direction feel firm. My #12: 將欲廢之，必固興之 — "about to abolish it, one must first raise it" (`quoted`); my list records that the words do not say who stretches, and my reading is that EJ raises it, in their own words, or the removal is not permitted.

8. **Change 4 — the suggestion and the acceptance are two records.** My #12 again: 將欲奪之，必固與之 — "about to seize it, one must first give it" (`quoted`) — the agent's case is given, the user's is the taking. My #3: 功遂身退，天之道 — "the work done, the self withdraws: this is the Way of heaven" (`quoted`) — once accepted or rejected, the suggestion stops attending to itself. Check: every accepted case names who suggested and who accepted; an acceptance with no suggestion record fails, and a suggestion with no acceptance fails. Price: two forms means two chances to leave a blank. My #6's 是以聖人猶難之，故終無難矣 — "so the sage still treats things as difficult, and therefore in the end has no difficulty" (`quoted`) — and my #19 (attributed): 人皆取實，己獨取虛 — "everyone takes the solid; he alone takes the empty" — the field nobody fills is the one the house rule is really about.

9. **Change 5 — the accepted list is re-read at the end of the task, cheaply.** My #7: 民之從事，常於幾成而敗之。慎終如始，則無敗事 — "People, in their undertakings, often fail when they are close to finishing. Careful at the end as at the beginning, then no undertaking fails" (`quoted`). Under EJ's direction the last thing touched is the requirement detail, and the first thing written is the use case — the failure shape my #7 names is the artifact nobody re-reads. Check: at the end, every accepted case is either covered by a check that has been seen failing or dropped by the user with a written reason; a case that vanishes silently fails. Price: it costs a pass over the list per task; if that pass is expensive it gets skipped, and a skipped re-read is worse than none, because the coverage claim still stands.

10. **T1 · must/should — two different things, and only one of them may be MUST.** Existence is one question (should); obedience is another (must). They are not a contradiction, but they cannot both be musts under the house rule. So: a requirement SHOULD exist; a requirement that *is* a safety bound MUST be obeyed; and "results must be valid for use cases" is realised as a per-case property — every accepted case has a comparison that can fail — which is a method rule and therefore a SHOULD. My #8 is the reason not to promote it: 知不知上，不知知病 — "knowing not-knowing: highest; not-knowing knowing: sickness" (`quoted`). A validity claim with no written comparison is not-knowing knowing.

11. **T2 · what the 80% counts over — conditional, and the main agent's reading is half right.** If a requirement exists, the units are its text and its fails-if, and nothing changes. If it does not, the denominator is the accepted use-case list — and here the user's decision does fix the list, but *after* the generator chose the size of the batch. That is the whole of the trial: the same requirement, 28 cases and 200 cases, raw coverage 46% and 20%, fixed units 80% both times. So the fixedness is downstream of the generator; what the user's acceptance fixes is the *content*, not the *count*. My #9: 欲上民，必以言下之。欲先民，必以身後之 — "wanting to be above the people, one must in speech put oneself below them; wanting to be ahead of them, one must in person put oneself behind them" (`quoted`) — the user judging200 items one by one is being ahead in person while saying it aloud200 times; the position does not survive the arithmetic. Report, never invent: suggested / accepted / declined per round, and recompute the count whenever the accepted list changes. House rule honoured — no number is invented; the trigger is a comparison (a round in which nothing was declined).

12. **T2 · the harder half: an acceptance that never declines is not a check.** My #8 again, from the other side: a user who accepts everything they have not read is 不知知. Concrete plant, from this run's own evidence: put the generated case that contradicted a settled decision into the suggestion batch. If acceptance rejects it, step 2 can fail and the house rule is met; if it accepts it, step 2 adds length and the coverage number is the generator's number with the user's signature on it. My #11: 弱之勝強，柔之勝剛，天下莫不知莫能行 — "the weak beats the strong, the soft beats the hard; everyone in the world knows it and no one can practise it" (`quoted`).

13. **T3 · who makes what — two makers, two forms.** The AI's case is evidence with a maker attached, not a maker (my reading, carried from my earlier answer in this run). My #10: 善故能勇，儉故能廣，不敢為天下先，故能成器長 — and its price, written in the words: 舍慈且勇，舍儉且廣，舍後且先，死矣 — "to give up being behind and yet be first, that is death" (`quoted`). So the agent does not set the count and does not mark its own case covered (my #13: 善有果而已，不敢以取強 — "the good one has a result and that is all; he does not dare use it to take strength", `quoted`). And the units? My #16: 知人者智，自知者明。勝人者有力，自勝者強 — "knowing others is clever; knowing oneself is clear; overcoming others takes force, overcoming oneself is strength" (`quoted`): if the agent that wrote the code also marks the case covered, the coverage is self-assessment. R11.1 already splits this — an independent reviewer can fail the marks — and the same split must apply to a use case, not only to a unit.

14. **The floor under all of it: no free money.** My #13: 戰勝，以喪禮處之 — "a victory in war is handled with the rites of mourning" (`quoted`) — every case dropped is recorded, not deleted, and my #4's 去甚，去奢，去泰 — "discards the extreme, the extravagant, the excessive" (`quoted`) — is what stops the accepted list becoming the 200-case list with a filter on top. Everything in "How we would know" below reuses the lists already generated in `coverage-trial/`; any new generation is a paid engine call and needs EJ's go-ahead (house rule).

## What I need to know

- **Does a work ever reach done with accepted use cases and no requirement?** Not given. If never, the SHOULD is decoration and change 2 is belt-and-braces; if often, change 2 is the difference between a coverage ratio and a silence, and it has to be built, not assumed.
- **When200 cases arrive, is that 200 decisions or one?** Not given, and it decides T2. If one, the accepted list cannot be a denominator and the ratio must count over units extracted from the accepted cases before any case is generated (my #6).
- **Who decides a use case is "covered"?** EJ's direction does not say. If it is the agent that wrote the code, my #16 applies and the number is self-assessment with a review step attached.
- **Is "results are valid for use cases" checked by a judge returning pass/fail, or read by a person?** Not given. If read by a person, my #8 applies and it is 不知知 until the comparison is written.
- **When EJ says "must", do they mean a safety bound or "required"?** The house rule reserves MUST for safety. The rewrite in change 3 depends on which.

## Costs and risks

- **Conditions per case cost EJ reading time they did not sign up for.** My #2 (知足不辱，知止不殆 — "knowing what is enough, one is not disgraced; knowing to stop, one is not endangered", `quoted`). The number of conditions per case is EJ's to set; I do not set it.
- **Two records per case means two chances to leave a field blank.** My #19 (attributed): 以深爲根，以約爲紀 — "he takes depth as the root and restraint as the rule" (`quoted`). Keeping the two records short is the restraint.
- **Dropping "use cases are must" from the wording makes the direction read as weaker than it is.** My #12: 將欲廢之，必固興之 (`quoted`) — the raise is owed, and my list records that the words do not say who does it.
- **Letting requirements be optional invites the "detail" reading**, where a requirement becomes elaboration that no test can fail — the same harm R12.4 was written to prevent. My #8: only by treating the sickness as a sickness is one not sick.
- **A cheap re-read still gets skipped if the list is long**, and a skipped re-read leaves the coverage claim standing — worse than no re-read. My #11 (天下莫不知莫能行): known and not practised.
- **Removing one MUST removes the sentence EJ liked.** My #14 (少則得，多則惑, `quoted`) is the price of fewer words, and the safety MUST is already in R11.1 rule 6, so nothing is lost except the emphasis.

## How we would know I was right

- **The no-fails-if plant.** Add one case with no comparison to the accepted list. The covered count must not rise. If it rises, change 1 is wrong and R11.1's rule 1 is already being broken in practice.
- **The contradiction plant.** The trial found a generated case that contradicted a settled decision; put it in the suggestion batch. Acceptance must be able to reject it. If nothing can be rejected in a whole round, step 2 is a formality and my T2 answer stands (my #8).
- **The decline count, over several rounds.** If the number declined is always zero, the acceptance is not a check and the 80% is the generator's number. This is a comparison, not a threshold, so no number is invented.
- **Reuse the existing lists, at no quota.** Feed the 28-case and 200-case lists into the accepted path and count units that appear in one and not the other. If both lists yield the same units — which the trial already suggests, since both surfaced the same three gaps — then the acceptance adds no information, and what protects coverage is the unit rule, not the acceptance.
- **The dropped case.** Remove one accepted case silently between the accepted list and the final report. If nothing fails, change 5 is not built and my #7's concern is confirmed.
- **The wording check.** If the rewritten direction still carries "use cases are must" as a method rule, requirement-check fails it and change 3 was not really made.
