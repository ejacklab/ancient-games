# Framing — Claude (Opus 5.5), blind

## 1. Is "one framework" better?

Yes. "Main product + framework" makes it sound like two projects. That invites building the method without the
referee, or the referee without the method. EJ's framing names the one loop that connects them (design → check →
feedback into design), and that loop is the reason the two belong in one repo.

Two things to check before the line goes in. Neither changes the answer:

- **Is the feedback loop built yet, or planned?** If no referee output is fed into the method today, the sentence
  states a goal as a fact. One word ("is meant to feed", or a pointer to the open finding) keeps it honest.
- **The skill is linked globally and runs in other projects without the referee.** That is fine, because a part can
  be used alone. But the sentence should not suggest that the method needs the referee to work.

## 2. Replacement sentence

> The Ancient Games is one framework for agent work: its workflow-design method (`.claude/skills/workflow-design/`)
> designs the workflow for an incoming task, its referee (`ancient_games/`) checks the work, and what the referee
> finds feeds back into the method — both drawing on the knowledge kept in `docs/`.
