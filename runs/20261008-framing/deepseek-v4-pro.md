# Framing verdict — deepseek-v4-pro

**1. Is EJ's framing better?**

Yes. The current split labels one part "the main product" and the other "the framework," which both demotes the referee to a subordinate and misnames the whole — the framework is the closed loop (task → method designs → referee checks → feedback back into the method), not the referee alone. The method and the referee are two parts of one loop, not two products, so "one framework with two parts" is the truer description.

**2. The replacement sentence**

> The Ancient Games is one framework: the workflow-design method (`.claude/skills/workflow-design/`) is the part that turns an incoming task into a designed workflow, and `ancient_games/` is the part that referees the work, with the referee's findings feeding back into the method.
