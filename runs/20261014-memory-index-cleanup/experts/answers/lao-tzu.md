# 老子 (Laozi) — which spots are answered by giving up a step

## Answer

Give up the step, and the spot stops being a question. I answer **yes** to Q1, D1, D4 and D5; **partly** to D2 and Q2; **no** to Q2's substance and **no** to D3, which is the one place this design must take something away rather than resolve anything. Four more spots I raise myself (N1–N4) are also subtraction candidates, each with one thing named as lost.

## How I decided

1. The question asks for spots that **stop being questions** when a step is taken away, so I looked first for steps that exist only to carry a decision: a merge someone else's session already made, a date check nobody asked for, a backlog nobody asked to read, a tool whose absence changes nothing. That is my **#1**, *"Take away, and take away again"* — 損之又損，以至於無為。無為而無不為。 — and its second clause is the test I applied to every "yes": with the step gone, **is anything left undone?** A "yes" that leaves undone work is not 無為, it is laziness wearing its clothes. Q1 leaves undone work (nothing is merged by this run); D1 leaves undone work (`31-02-` passes); D4 leaves undone work (item 5's closure line stays); D5 leaves undone work (independence). D3 fails this test, which is why it gets a "no".
2. **Q1** I decided by my **#3**, *"Stop before it is full; when the work is done, withdraw"* — 功遂身退，天之道. That other session finished its work and withdrew; taking its branch and merging it is this run reaching for what is already somebody else's. And my **#1**'s own sentence fits it exactly: 取天下常以無事，及其有事，不足以取天下。 — *"The world is always taken by having no business; once one has business, one is not enough to take the world."* The start point is taken by not doing the merge. I add my **#8** — 知不知上 — because we do not know whether `d990dab` will ever reach master, and a design must not be built on that guess.
3. **Q2** I decided by my **#6**, *"Act while it is small and easy; treat it as difficult"* — 為之於未有，治之於未亂。 Order it before it is in disorder. The disorder here is the spec: N1 is a blind test writer who works *from the words*, so unconfirmed words do not fail loudly, they fail later and everywhere. What I subtract is the sequencing, not the confirmation — N0's mechanical prep does not depend on the wording, so it does not wait either.
4. **D1 and D4** I decided by my **#2**, *"Know what is enough; know to stop"* — 禍莫大於不知足。咎莫大於欲得。 No fault is greater than wanting to get. A calendar check is wanted, not needed; so is the rest of the backlog. The loss is written above in one line each, which is what my #2 asks: the enough is named, not argued.
5. **D2** I decided by my **#14**, *"Do not display yourself; do not contend"* — 不自見故明。夫唯不爭，故天下莫能與之爭。 A check written as one loop over all entries, with no branch on status, is true in every status by construction — nothing to display and nothing to argue about. (My reading: this is an application of the restraint, not the text's words.) The cost is thinness, and thinness is my **#8**: we would not know a status branch had appeared until a retired entry held a bad id, and nothing observes that, because `project-memory/` exists in no product.
6. **D3** I decided by my **#8** again, and here it is the load-bearing use. A bare `=======` — we do not know what it is. It is equally a Markdown heading underline and half of a conflict block. *"To know that you do not know is best"* — 知不知上，不知知病 — and *"only by treating sickness as sickness is one not sick."* A tool that flags `=======` claims to know it is a marker; it does not know. So the marker set stays at SoT K6's two, and the one fixture that says `=======` alone passes is the test that holds this. My **#7** — 慎終如始，則無敗事 — says why that fixture must not be given up: people fail when the work is nearly done, and this tool's first real use is far in the future, when no run is watching.
7. **D5** I decided by my **#13**, *"Achieve the result and stop"* — 善有果而已，不敢以取強。Result, and no forcing; he does not dare use the result to take strength. N5 already achieves the result: the traceability table and its "no empty cell" check. Using a second tool to prove the table proves more than it proves is taking strength from it. And my **#3** — 功成而弗居。夫唯弗居，是以不去。 The work is achieved and it does not dwell in it — an extra check that only re-reads the same table is the tool dwelling in its own result.
8. **N1** I decided by my **#19**, *"Where others take the front, take the rear; restraint as the rule"* — 以深為根，以約爲紀 (*attributed*, 莊子·天下's description). The coder yields rather than handing the wheel to the engine that wrote the tests it is failing; restraint, not escalation, is the rule here. If the attempts go too, my **#13** pays for it: 果而已 — a result and stop — and the price of stopping is that stopping can be premature.
9. **N2 and N3** I decided by my **#2** again, against my **#7**. The second baseline run is cheap now and expensive later (N2, after the tests are frozen), so it is subtracted only on a clean first run and a repo with no flaky history — that is a fact I was not given, so the verdict is conditional. For the old mutants, what is subtracted is proof that was never about this diff; what must not be subtracted is the old **survivor**, because a survivor is the one place where a re-run can still find something. My **#8** names the price: the report then says "applicable mutants killed", and must not say 32/32.
10. **N4** I decided by my **#7** and my **#13** together. The careful-at-the-end rule is why I do **not** subtract N4's second review on a plant miss, and the result-and-stop rule is why I do subtract the re-read after a fix — a fix is a small diff against a finding that carries `file:line`, and the gate plus N3 already re-run against it.
11. Finally, my own bias, named because the method names it: **the method warns that 老子's rules reward subtraction** — *"never dropped silently: 老子 named the risk himself."* So I name it: three of my four "yes" verdicts rest on rules whose reward is taking things away. The counterweight is D3, where the same rules say the one cheap fixture is the thing that must stay. A list of seven subtractions with no "no" in it would be my rules talking, not the design's facts.

## Costs and risks

- **Q1 — do not merge.** If `d990dab` is later rewritten or abandoned, this branch carries a commit EJ never asked for; and the review diff is taken against the wrong base, so "must not change" is measured against `d990dab`, not master. *Item: my #3, #14.* Undoing it costs one `git branch -D`, which is why I can propose it.
- **Q2 — N0 no longer waits.** If EJ's answer changes the wording, the two pinned hashes are stale and `traceability.md` starts from re-taken evidence. *Item: my #6 (order it before it is in disorder) — this is the price of ordering it in parallel.*
- **D1 — no calendar check.** `00-00-aaaaaa-0000` and `31-02-xxxxxx-2026` pass silently, and the error surfaces at index time, in a product that does not exist yet. *Item: my #2.*
- **D2 — no status matrix.** A future `if status == "active"` guard in the marker or id check turns no test red. *Item: my #8 — we would not know it was there.*
- **D3 — nothing given up.** The cost of keeping is one fixture, and the honest cost is that this fixture is the *only* thing holding D3: `trace_check.py` finds B2.7 whether or not `=======` is tested. A one-fixture check is a thin guard. *Item: my #7.*
- **D4 — never open the backlog.** EJ loses the side view of what this run made cheap; and if the item-5 closure line is cut as collateral, a closed backlog item reads as an open debt. *Item: my #1 (損之又損) — and the second 損 is knowing which line not to cut.*
- **D5 — no evaluator.** If the evaluator is a different check and not a re-reading, requirement coverage loses its only independent reader. *Item: my #8, and the open question above.*
- **N1 — no Codex exit.** A fourth attempt that would have landed goes to EJ instead; and each exit that fires dilutes the blind/different-engine separation the design claims. *Item: my #13 — 果而已; the price of stopping is that it can stop too soon.*
- **N2 — one baseline run.** A flaky test is found after the tests are frozen, inside `baseline.json`, and the gate's baseline comparison can flap on the reruns. *Item: my #7.*
- **N3 — drop the untouched old mutants.** The mutant verdict rests partly on "the diff does not touch that line" — an argument, not a run — and the earlier run already had one survivor, so this design is not the place to grow comfortable. *Item: my #8.*
- **N4 — no reviewer re-read.** A fix that passes the gate and N3 but misses the reviewer's meaning is caught by EJ at acceptance, later. *Item: my #7.*
- **This answer as a whole.** Every "yes" leaves EJ a smaller net, and the method says a spot I call droppable is a *question for EJ*, never a removal. Nothing above is done on my say-so.

## How we would know I was right

1. **Q1:** `state.md` shows N0 complete while Q2 is still open and no merge commit exists anywhere in the run; the branch's first parent is `d990dab`. If N0 cannot finish without an answer about the merge, my verdict was wrong.
2. **D5:** at N5 the traceability table has no empty cell **with the evaluator never having run**. That is the table's own check and it is directly observable in `report.md`; the ledger's coverage row then reads "requirement ids covered once, by hand" — coverage intact, independence gone. If a later run's evaluator finds an id the table missed, my verdict was wrong.
3. **D4:** the run's whole evidence set mentions exactly one backlog item (item 5, in N5). Count the references; more than one means the boundary leaked.
4. **D3:** `tests/test_memory_index.py` contains a test whose only claim is that `=======` alone passes. Delete it and nothing else in the design notices — that is the measure, and it is a measure of the design, not of the reviewer.
5. **N2/N3/N4:** the mutant table lists every skipped old mutant with its reason, the count of skipped ones is stated rather than folded into "32/32", and N4 runs once. Then: does the run still reach the three-part stop — new criteria green and shown able to fail, baseline green, must-not holds? The method already asks for this count after the first three designs (`docs/WORKFLOW_DESIGN_METHOD.md` 3.3): count the spots the experts added, dropped or changed, and whether the run still reached its stop. If runs that took these subtractions keep reaching the stop, the subtraction was subtraction; if they stop short, I rewarded taking things away where the method needed coverage, and that is what the warning about me was for.
