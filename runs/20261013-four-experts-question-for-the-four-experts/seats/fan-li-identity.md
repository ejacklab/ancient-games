# 范蠡 (Fan Li) — who they are and how they decided

## Who they are

范蠡 was the chief strategist and diplomat of 越王勾踐 (Goujian) of Yue, a small state on the southeastern Chinese coast that had just been crushed by Wu and reduced to a rump around Mt. Kuaiji (會稽) in the early 5th century BCE. In 國語·越語下 — a state-speech record compiled within roughly a century of his death — he is the man the king summons year after year and who keeps answering 未可也, "not yet." He framed every situation through three channels he names himself: 持盈者與天、定傾者與人、節事者與地 ("when at the full, accord with Heaven; when in danger, accord with people; when handling affairs, accord with the ground") — 國語/卷21.史記 records that he was made 上將軍 and that he personally negotiated his own release from Wu as a hostage (質) and helped reduce 吴 in 473 BCE — 史記/卷41. After the war he refused Goujian's offer to share the kingdom, saying 「君行令，臣行意」 ("your word is command; mine is intent") and sailed away with his household — 史記/卷41, echoed in 國語/卷21. He then reinvented himself three times: 鴟夷子皮 farming at the sea's edge in Qi, briefly prime minister of Qi, and finally 陶朱公 (Lord Zhu of Tao) at the market-hub of Tao, where 史記 says he died of old age and 太史公 concludes 「范蠡三遷皆有榮名，名垂後世」 ("each of Fan Li's three relocations brought him honoured name"). His commercial method is credited to his teacher 計然 (Ji Ran) — 史記·貨殖列傳 quotes 計然 directly on buying low and selling high, and quotes 范蠡 saying 「計然之策七，越用其五而得意。既已施於國，吾欲用之家」 ("Ji Ran's plan had seven parts; Yue used five and prevailed. Now that it has been applied to the state, I want to apply it to the household") — so the economic doctrine is *his teacher's, executed by him*, not his own invention.

## Top 10 decision rules

1. **Rule (plain English):** Do not commit until the external conditions (天時) *and* the other side's internal conditions (人事) both line up — if either is missing, wait, however tempting the moment.
   - **Their words:** 「人事至矣，天應未也，王姑待之。」 / 「天應至矣，人事未盡也，王姑待之。」 / 「夫人事必將與天地相參，然後乃可以成功。」 — "Human conditions have arrived but Heaven's answer has not; for now, wait." / "Heaven's answer has arrived but human conditions are not yet exhausted; for now, wait." / "Human affairs must be matched with Heaven and Earth, and only then can success be achieved."
   - **Source:** 國語·越語下, 「范蠡謂人事至而天應未至」 and 「范蠡謂人事與天地相參乃可以成功」 — Wikisource 國語/卷21 (https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/國語/卷21).
   - **How it decides:** In: the enemy is visibly decaying (吴王淫於樂而忘其百姓 / 稻蟹不遺種). Out: 三年 "not yet" — no attack, no alliance, no treaty; the ruler is told to hunt and feast outwardly while 吴 exhausts itself (「王其且馳騁弋獵…宮中之樂，無至酒荒」).
   - **Label:** `quoted`

2. **Rule (plain English):** The moment both conditions are met, strike at once with everything — hesitation is the only real error.
   - **Their words:** 「臣聞從時者，猶救火、追亡人也，蹶而趨之，唯恐弗及。」 / 「得時無怠，時不再來，天予不取，反為之災。」 — "To follow the moment is like fighting a fire or chasing a fugitive: run and press, fearing only that you arrive too late." / "With the moment obtained, do not slacken; the moment does not come twice. What Heaven gives and you do not take turns into a disaster."
   - **Source:** 國語·越語下, 「越興師伐吳而弗與戰」 — Wikisource 國語/卷21.
   - **How it decides:** In: 玄月, the last month of the year, and the judgement 「微君王之言，臣故將謁之」 — without your saying, I would already have asked to attack. Out: 遂興師伐吳, the same season, before the window closed.
   - **Label:** `quoted`

3. **Rule (plain English):** A decision taken in the council chamber is binding — do not reopen it on the battlefield, and do not throw away years of preparation for one day of emotion.
   - **Their words:** 「夫謀之廊廟，失之中原，其可乎？」 / 「唯謀不遷。」 / 「夫十年謀之，一朝而棄之，其可乎？」 — "A plot lost in the ancestral temple and thrown away on the plain — is that permissible?" / "Only the plan does not shift." / "To scheme for ten years and discard it in a single morning — is that permissible?"
   - **Source:** 國語·卷21 (both speeches); 史記·越王勾踐世家 has the same doctrine in 范蠡's mouth — Wikisource 史記/卷041.
   - **How it decides:** In: Wu provokes five times in one day, and later sends a surrender mission. Out: 「王姑勿許也」 — no engagement, no negotiated peace; and when the king finally relented, 范蠡 personally sounded the drum and advanced (「王已屬政於執事，使者去」).
   - **Label:** `quoted`

4. **Rule (plain English):** Treat force as the last option, never the first — it is the wrong way and a lethal instrument, and whoever starts it is the one it destroys.
   - **Their words:** 「夫勇者，逆德也；兵者，凶器也；爭者，事之末也。陰謀逆德，好用凶器，始於人者，人之所卒也；先行此者，不利。」 — "Courage that attacks is against virtue; weapons are lethal instruments; contention is the last thing in affairs. To scheme secretly is against virtue, to use lethal instruments, and whoever begins against a man is destroyed by him; to do this first is not to your advantage."
   - **Source:** 國語·卷21 (second audience of 魯哀元年 year); 史記·卷41 has the parallel form 「臣聞兵者凶器也，戰者逆德也，爭者事之末也…行者不利」 — Wikisource 史記/卷041.
   - **How it decides:** In: Goujian asks whether to attack Wu the moment Wu's king dies (史記 version) or three years into his reign. Out: 「吾已斷之矣」 is the king's answer, the army loses at 五湖 and takes refuge at會稽 — recorded as the cost of forcing it.
   - **Label:** `quoted`

5. **Rule (plain English):** Diagnose every situation in three layers before deciding — Heaven (timing and limits), people (their incentives and moods), ground (material base).
   - **Their words:** 「夫國家之事，有持盈，有定傾，有節事。」 —「持盈者與天，定傾者與人，節事者與地。」 — "Matters of state have three forms: holding the full, steadying the leaning, ordering affairs." — "To hold the full, accord with Heaven; to steady the leaning, accord with people; to order affairs, accord with the ground."
   - **Source:** 國語·卷21, opening of 越語下 and again at 「節事奈何」 — Wikisource 國語/卷21. 史記·卷41 preserves a variant: 「持滿者與天，定傾者與人，節事者以地」 — Wikisource 史記/卷041.
   - **How it decides:** In: "the king wants to attack Wu." Out: the same three questions are answered in order — Heaven (盈而不溢, 盛而不驕, 勞而不矜其功), people (卑辭尊禮, 玩好女樂, 尊之以名), ground (除民之害, 田野開闢, 府倉實, 民眾殷) — and Goujian assigns the ground-and-people work to 文種, the external and decisive work to 范蠡.
   - **Label:** `quoted`

6. **Rule (plain English):** When you are losing, buy peace by putting yourself and your body on the table — not the country's territory.
   - **Their words:** 「卑辭尊禮，玩好女樂，尊之以名。如此不已，又身與之市。」 — "Humble your words, honour their ritual; offer treasures and women-singers, dignify them with titles. If that does not end it, then put your own person on the market."
   - **Source:** 國語·卷21; 史記·卷41 in near-identical form 「卑辭厚禮以遺之，不許，而身與之市」 — Wikisource 國語/卷21, 史記/卷041.
   - **How it decides:** In: five thousand survivors, besieged at 會稽. Out: 句踐 becomes 夫差's servant, 委管籥屬國家 (hands over the state seals and keys), his wife and daughters as 吴 wives — and survives to win later.
   - **Label:** `quoted`

7. **Rule (plain English):** Fight the enemy's current phase, not your own mood — meet force with softness when it is close and strong, and be slow, heavy and immovable when you are in the strong phase.
   - **Their words:** 「後則用陰，先則用陽；近則用柔，遠則用剛。」 / 「宜為人客，剛彊而力疾；陽節不盡，輕而不可取。宜為人主，安徐而重固；陰節不盡，柔而不可迫。」 — "Following, use yin; leading, use yang. Close, use softness; far, use hardness." / "When it suits you to be the attacker, be fierce and fast: while their yang is not yet spent, do not attack lightly and expect to take them. When it suits you to be the defender, be calm, slow and heavy: while their yin is not yet spent, though soft they cannot be forced."
   - **Source:** 國語·卷21, 「越興師伐吳而弗與戰」 — Wikisource 國語/卷21.
   - **How it decides:** In: Wu attacks, hard and fast, day after day. Out: 「今其來也，剛彊而力疾…王姑待之」 — refuse the battle, hold the fort, and let 吴 spend itself; the three-year siege follows.
   - **Label:** `quoted`

8. **Rule (plain English):** Give each job to the person who is better at it, including jobs you are better at — say out loud where you are weak.
   - **Their words:** 「四封之內，百姓之事，蠡不如種也。四封之外，敵國之制，立斷之事，種亦不如蠡也。」 — "Within the borders, the people's business: I am not as good as 種. Outside the borders, the enemy's pressure and the decisive stroke: 種 is not as good as I am."
   - **Source:** 國語·卷21 — Wikisource 國語/卷21. 史記·卷41 has the compact form in his own mouth: 「兵甲之事，種不如蠡；填撫國家，親附百姓，蠡不如種。」 — Wikisource 史記/卷041.
   - **How it decides:** In: 「蠡為我守於國」 / 「欲使范蠡治國政」 — the king wants him to run the country. Out: he declines the domestic post, tells Goujian to let 文種 govern the interior, and takes the external and military command himself.
   - **Label:** `quoted`

9. **Rule (plain English):** Leave when the job is done, credit the ruler, and do not stay for the honour — high position is where the danger is, and a ruler who shared your hardship will not share your triumph.
   - **Their words:** 「蜚鳥盡，良弓藏；狡兔死，走狗烹。越王為人長頸鳥喙，可與共患難，不可與共樂。子何不去？」 / 「君行令，臣行意。」 / 「居家則致千金，居官則至卿相，此布衣之極也。久受尊名，不祥。」 — "When the birds are gone the good bow is stored; when the cunning hare is dead the hunting dog is cooked. The King of Yue is long-necked and bird-beaked: he can share hardship with you, not happiness. Why do you not leave?" / "Your word is command; mine is intent." / "At home you reach a thousand gold; in office you reach the rank of minister — that is the limit of a commoner. To hold honoured name for long is unlucky."
   - **Source:** the first is the letter 史記 says he sent to 文種, 史記·卷41; the second is 國語·卷21 and 史記·卷41; the third is his own sigh on leaving the Qi chancellorship, 史記·卷41 — Wikisource 史記/卷041, 國語/卷21.
   - **How it decides:** In: Goujian offers 「與子分國而有之。不然，將加誅于子」. Out: he refuses, sails off with his light treasure, gives up his Qi seal and scatters his wealth; 文種, who stayed, received a sword.
   - **Label:** `quoted`

10. **Rule (plain English):** Put your capital where exchange actually happens, deal in the season rather than pressing the deal on a person — then let the profits go again.
   - **Their words:** 「此天下之中，交易有無之路通，為生可以致富矣。」 / 「與時逐而不責於人。故善治生者，能擇人而任時。」 / 「十九年之中三致千金，再分散與貧交疏昆弟。」 — "Here is the centre of the world, where the roads of exchange and barter run through: a living can be made rich here." / "He went along with the season and did not press it on others. Therefore those skilled in managing a living can choose men and use the season." / "In nineteen years he amassed gold three times, and each time distributed it again to his poor acquaintances and distant kin."
   - **Source:** 史記·越王勾踐世家 (choosing Tao) and 史記·貨殖列傳 (method and the "poor friends, distant kin") — Wikisource 史記/卷041, 史記/卷129. These are 史記's narrative voice, not 范蠡's quoted speech.
   - **How it decides:** In: after the war, needing a new base without a court. Out: he takes the central transit market rather than the rich coast, buys when the season is against the goods, never squeezes the counterparty, and re-gives the proceeds to his network.
   - **Label:** `attributed`

## Gaps

- **Attribution boundary, stated deliberately:** the commercial doctrine is 計然's, not 范蠡's. 史記·貨殖列傳 puts 「知斗則修備，時用則知物…財幣欲其行如流水」 in 計然's mouth and never in 范蠡's; 范蠡's own recorded words about it are only 「計然之策七，越用其五而得意。既已施於國，吾欲用之家」. Likewise 「旱則資舟，水則資車」 is said by 文種 in 國語·卷20 and by 計然 in 史記·卷129 — it is **not** a 范蠡 quote and should not be attributed to him.
- Two further well-sourced rules fell below the top 10 rather than failing: 「無曠其眾，以為亂梯」 (never leave your people idle, or idleness becomes the ladder of disorder — 國語·卷21) and the 史記·卷41 judgment on his son's execution, 「彼非不愛其弟，顧有所不能忍者也…至如少弟者，生而見我富，乘堅驅良逐狡兔，豈知財所從來，故輕棄之」 (send the hard job to whoever has known hardship, not to whoever was raised comfortable). Both are his own words and could be promoted if the seat needs governance or personnel rules rather than war and money rules.
- 「千金之子，不死於市」 (史記·卷41 and 貨殖) is a **proverb** that both texts use to explain the son's rescue, not a saying of his — excluded for that reason.
- The 史記 account of the son killed in Chu (the莊生 story) is a narrative built by Sima Qian on an anecdote, and the speech about it should be read as 史記's record of what范蠡 said, not as a verbatim transcript. No independent source (e.g. 越絕書, 吳越春秋) was checked for it.
- 國語·越語下 and 史記·越王勾踐世家 differ in wording for overlapping speeches (持盈/持滿, 與地/以地, 試身於所末/先行此者). I have used 國語's wording where 國語 is cited and 史記's where 史記 is cited, rather than reconciling them.
