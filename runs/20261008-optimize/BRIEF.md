been heavily edited over weeks — rules added, deleted, reframed. It has almost certainly accumulated dead weight.

Find, each with its section number or line and a one-line reason:

1. **DUPLICATES** — the same concept stated in two places (candidates: the "who fills it" rule, "one word one job",

Give the **5–10 highest-value cuts or merges**, not a full audit. Do NOT rewrite the doc — produce the list, ordered
by value. If the method is already lean, say so and name the one thing you'd still trim.
