# 老子 (Laozi) — does the agent know memory by itself, and should the method say more?

## Answer

**Q1: No — not by its own judgement, and with one qualification that decides the whole thing.** The reads that happen are the reads the harness forces (report §1, "Across the tools": every vendor forces the index read). What is left to the agent is *not measured* (§2, §7 Q1) or measured poor (E3 self-authored skills below the no-skills baseline, E5 add-all worse than a fixed store, E6 rewriting collapsing 18,282 → 122 tokens). The qualification: for "what to keep, what to leave out" the answer needs no measurement of the model at all, because the failure has a name you can check without asking the agent — and all three rows above are that one illness. So: the timing is given from outside, and the keeping is unchecked from within.

**Q2: Partly — one change to §4, and it is not details of how agents use memory.** Replace §4's project-memory paragraph (L494–510) with a pointer to `docs/memory-system/SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md`, and in that same paragraph put one sentence with two halves. The first half is not mine and I do not improve it: *a node's memory arrives by brief, import or start hook, never as a request to read it* (孫子's O8; 範蠡's O1). The second half is mine, and none of O6, O7, O8 says it: **what a node writes is kept only when the run's end, or a later reader, names it; until then it stays in that task's journal.** All three proposals add what *reaches* a node. None says what *leaves*. Conditional: if EJ will not touch SoT's W4.1 ("no owners and no approval gate"), the second half cannot stand as written, and my change reduces to the pointer plus the first half — I say so below rather than pretend otherwise.

## How I decided

1. Report §1's split is the timing, and my #18 is the rule about timing, `attributed`: 且君子得其時則駕，不得其時則蓬累而行 — "a gentleman, when his time comes, rides out; when it does not, he goes off like drifting tumbleweed." *Attributed* means this is Sima Qian's report of Laozi speaking, not the book's words. The words do not say the timing is the agent's own. That the harness sets it is my reading of §1's own sentence, "no tool lets the model choose *whether to read the index*".

2. The same item ends 「吾所以告子，若是而已」 — "what I have to tell you is this, and only this." That is why my change is one sentence and not a section. Everything else waits for a run.

3. Report §2's E3, E5 and E6 are one disease under three names, and my #2 names it before any of them was measured, `quoted`: 禍莫大於不知足；咎莫大於欲得。故知足之足，常足矣 — "no calamity is greater than not knowing what is enough; no fault is greater than wanting to get. So the enough of knowing enough is a lasting enough." So the "what to keep" half of Q1 is answered by *counting what leaves*, not by scoring the model. Nobody has measured the model's memory quality (§7 Q1), and my rule says you need not: if nothing ever leaves the store, the store is already in the illness whatever the agent's reasoning was. My #16, `quoted`, 知足者富 — "one who knows what is enough is rich" — is the same statement in eight parallel clauses, not a ladder (that is my list's own note on #16).

4. Report §2 E4, E7 and E10, and §5's "On EJ's premise", all say more memory is not better by itself. My #1 says it harder, `quoted`: 為學日益，為道日損。損之又損，以至於無為。無為而無不為 — "pursuing learning, one gains daily; pursuing the Way, one loses daily. Lose and lose again, until one arrives at not-doing. Not doing, and nothing is left undone." Applied to memory (my reading): the store's job is to get *smaller* until what is left still does the work. So the direction of the change is subtraction, which is also EJ's own standing rule that a mechanism which is not needed is removed.

5. Report §5, on O6, O7, O8: each adds what reaches a node; O8 adds what a node must not receive. My #19 is the emptiness itself, `attributed` (莊子·天下, the 天下 author's description of Lao Dan): 無藏也，故有餘，巋然而有餘 … 以深為根，以約為紀 — "hoarding nothing, he has more than enough, abundantly … he takes depth as the root and restraint as the rule." Surplus comes from the empty. And its rule is restraint. So: the emptiness has to be *in the method*, and restraint says it is the last thing added, not the first.

6. My #17 is the shape of the thing, `quoted`: 三十輻共一轂，當其無，有車之用 … 故有之以為利，無之以為用 — "in its nothing lies the cart's use … what is there makes for benefit; what is not there makes for use." My list's own note on #17: the words do not say to remove anything; that would be an application, so the removal here is my reading. Read that way it settles孫子's O8 objection and mine at once: the *use* is in the absence, and an empty brief has benefit and no use. So the empty half must be sized by what the node needs, not by what is dangerous — which is why my sentence says a *named* write, not a *refused* one.

7. Report §4's layout disagreement (§4's three files against SoT's `entries/`): all three earlier experts resolve it to SoT, and my reason is my #3, `quoted`: 功成而弗居。夫唯弗居，是以不去 — "the work is achieved and he does not dwell in it. Just because he does not dwell in it, it does not leave." §4's paragraph is the dwelling-in of a design that is already accepted and already complete in SoT. Keeping both is the hoarding my #2 warns of, 同 second clause: 多藏必厚亡 — "much hoarding must mean heavy loss." That the *copies* are the problem is my reading.

8. Where the second half has to sit, and why not in SoT: SoT's "When to write" (L113–119; W5.1–W5.3) says *when* to write, and K5.2 says the agent "writes what is worth keeping as entries" — the writer chooses. K4.1–K4.2 retire an entry only when some reader finds a mismatch (Rd5). W4.1 says anyone may write with no owner and no gate. So nothing in SoT says when the *keeping* stops, and the choice sits with whoever wants to keep it. That is my reading of those four lines, and it is the gap my second half fills. It uses a pattern §4 already has — `lessons.md` today is "any node proposes, the COO appends" (L503) — so it adds a clause, not a mechanism.

9. What I do **not** touch, and why that is not an oversight: O3, O4, O5 and Linus's per-node contract. Method §4 L514 already says "Not verified: Codex's memory and subagent features beyond `AGENTS.md`". My #8 is about that, `quoted`: 聖人不病，以其病病，是以不病 — "the sage is not sick because he treats the sickness as a sickness; therefore he is not sick." Naming the ignorance is the treatment, so a named unknown needs no new sentence; it needs one run watched. That no sentence is needed is my reading, and it is a subtraction, which is my whole direction.

10. On EJ's premise ("memory and information are the two most important factors"): I do not dispute that memory matters, and I do not rank it — nobody measured the ranking (report §5). What I will not do is treat the ranking as a reason to add. My #4 is the limit, `quoted`: 為者敗之，執者失之 — "one who acts on it spoils it; one who holds on to it loses it." Which of EJ's memory the words govern is my reading, and I say it as a limit on my own change, not on his premise: this sentence touches only what a node *writes*. Nothing that SoT's Rd2.1 loads at session start and nothing Rd4.1 requires before an edit is in its scope. Gripping those is losing them.

## What I need to know

1. **Will EJ change SoT's W4.1 and K5.2?** If yes, the second half stands as written and one state is added to SoT (written but not named). If no, then my second half is impossible as stated — anyone may keep what they write — and my change becomes孫子's O8 plus the pointer, and I have said so in the Answer. This is EJ's call on an accepted requirement, not mine.
2. **Which point is "the run's end"?** SoT has three write times (W5.1 immediately, W5.3 when the work is complete, W5.2 at the end of a task). I was not told which one names memory, and I will not fill it (method item 12; `requirement-check`).
3. **Has anything ever been removed from EJ's own memory — an entry retired, a store pruned?** The report does not say. If nothing has, the second half fixes a disease no run has met, and my #2 puts a price on it: 甚愛必大費 — "great fondness must mean great expense." Every run keeps growing until someone notices.
4. **Is SoT going to be built before the next workflow that needs project memory** (report Q13)? If not, 範蠡's interim applies to the pointer half.
5. **Do the next designs use non-Claude nodes** (report Q12)? If not, the first half is nearly free and its cost is the brief tokens alone.

## Costs and risks

1. **The narrowing may omit a needed entry.** My #11, `quoted`: 弱之勝強，柔之勝剛，天下莫不知莫能行 — "the weak beats the strong, the soft beats the hard; everyone in the world knows it and no one can practise it." My #11 is a mindset, not an order, so this is my reading: the direction is right and the execution is where it fails, and the price is paid by the node that needed what was not named. 孫子's cost, "narrowing can omit a needed entry", is the same price under my item.
2. **The clause lands where undertakings fail.** My #7, `quoted`: 民之從事，常於幾成而敗之。慎終如始，則無敗事 — "people, in their undertakings, often fail when they are close to finishing; careful at the end as at the beginning, then no undertaking fails." Guodian 丙 puts it sharper in my list's own quotation: 人之敗也，恒於其且成也败之 — "people's failing always comes when they are about to succeed." A promotion step sits after the last check passed. If nobody is at the end to name anything, the store keeps growing silently and the rule looks satisfied. *Not measured.*
3. **It collides with an accepted requirement.** SoT W4.1: "no owners and no human approval gate." My sentence narrows who *promotes*, not who may write — that distinction is my reading, and a reviewer may well reject it as approval in disguise. If it is rejected, drop the second half; do not rewrite it into something larger.
4. **A rule that names nothing is hoarding in a new form.** My #2 again: 多藏必厚亡. If the first three workflows promote zero entries and nothing was ever wrong with what they left out, the sentence is 多藏 without benefit and should be deleted. The price of keeping it to find out is item 3 in "What I need to know".
5. **Removing §4's paragraph costs what is already spent.** My #3: 功遂身退，天之道 — "the work done, the self withdraws: this is the Way of heaven." The time spent learning §4's three-file layout is not recovered by deleting the text, and if SoT is unbuilt (F1) the pointer leads to rules with no store behind them.
6. **The paragraph must stop growing.** My #19's second clause, 以約為紀 — restraint as the rule. If §4 and App. B acquire another memory sentence without a measured harm behind it, restraint has been broken and this proposal is the first instance of the fault.
7. **One thing about the method this exposes, not the memory.** §4 L482 and `SKILL.md` L91–93 both name the state template and both point at one file, `docs/workflow-templates/state.md`; the skill's own copy under `assets/templates/` is byte-identical today (same md5) and nothing checks it. Two copies, no check, no measured harm. I report it under my #1 (subtraction) and I do **not** propose a check for it: that is a second mechanism, and it is not the change I was asked for. It belongs to whoever owns the method.
