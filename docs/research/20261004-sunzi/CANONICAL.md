# 孫子兵法 — canonical principles

### 1. Decide go or no-go from a written comparison made before you commit, and only commit when it already favours you
- **Text**: 兵者，國之大事，死生之地，存亡之道，不可不察也。……夫未戰而廟算勝者，得算多也；未戰而廟算不勝者，得算少也。多算勝，少算不勝，而況於無算乎！ — "War is the great affair of state, the ground of life and death, the way of survival and ruin; it must not go unexamined. … One who wins the temple calculation before battle has the greater tally; one who does not has the lesser. More tally wins, less does not — how much less, none at all." (計篇, Laying Plans)
- **Basis**: VERIFIED (source: Chinese Wikisource 孫子兵法, 始計第一 — fetched by minimax and deepseek)
- **Reading**: The opening declares war the gravest affair, one that "must not go unexamined," and 廟算 is that examination: a pre-campaign count of factors done in the ancestral temple, whose arithmetic is itself the predictor. 得算多 is ambiguous — "more factors scored in our favour" or "reckoned more thoroughly" — and both readings support the rule. The claim is comparative and quantitative, not a promise: more reckoning beats less, and none loses.
- **Design rule**: Before dispatching any agent, write the assessment against named criteria (objective, inputs, check, stop condition, budget) and the go/no-go that follows from it. A plan that cannot be scored is not started.
- **Violated by**: Spawning agents first and reading the first output back as evidence about whether the task was feasible.
- **Sources**: all three — claude-opus-5-5 (#1, RECALLED), minimax-m3.1-flash (#1, VERIFIED), deepseek-v4-pro (#2, VERIFIED). Agreed. (Claude also recalled 勝兵先勝而後求戰 into this principle; I moved it to #6, where the chapter places it.)

### 2. Bound the duration: value an ending over dragging on
- **Text**: 故兵貴勝，不貴久。 — "So in war, value victory; do not value duration." (作戰篇, Waging War). Claude also recalled, unverified, the sharper companion 兵聞拙速，未睹巧之久也 — "one has heard of clumsy speed; one has not seen cleverness that lasts."
- **Basis**: VERIFIED for 兵貴勝，不貴久 (source: Chinese Wikisource 孫子兵法, 作戰第二 — fetched by minimax). The 拙速 wording is RECALLED (claude only, unverified).
- **Reading**: The chapter argues that a prolonged campaign is ruinous — it lists the daily cost of an army in the field and concludes no prolonged war ever benefited a state. 兵貴勝，不貴久 is the slogan of that cost argument, so minimax's reading (the chapter is about expense) is primary; claude's reading (speed even at the cost of finesse) is a legitimate gloss of the same line and of the recalled 拙速 sentence. Both point the same way for design: an ending is worth more than continued refinement.
- **Design rule**: Every loop and every run gets a hard cap (iterations, budget, or time) and a stop condition, set before it starts. When the cap is hit the loop stops and reports its state; it does not ask for "one more pass."
- **Violated by**: A verify-and-repair loop that runs until the reviewer is satisfied, with no limit on rounds.
- **Sources**: claude-opus-5-5 (#5, RECALLED) and minimax-m3.1-flash (#3, VERIFIED, cost reading); deepseek-v4-pro did not carry it (its stop-condition point sits in #14). The three do not fully agree on what the line means — speed versus cost — and I read cost as the chapter's primary argument.

### 3. Know both the task and your own executors; half-knowledge is a coin flip
- **Text**: 知彼知己，百戰不殆；不知彼而知己，一勝一負；不知彼不知己，每戰必殆。 — "Know the other and know yourself, and in a hundred battles you will not be imperilled; know yourself but not the other, one win one loss; know neither, and every battle is peril." (謀攻篇, Attack by Stratagem)
- **Basis**: VERIFIED (source: Chinese Wikisource 孫子兵法, 謀攻第三 — fetched by deepseek; minimax quoted the same line in its caution section)
- **Reading**: The promise is 不殆 — not being imperilled — not guaranteed victory; the graded scale (full knowledge, half knowledge, none) makes the two objects of knowledge separately necessary. 地形篇 adds a third term (知彼知己，勝乃不殆；知天知地，勝乃可全), so "know both sides" is the top of a ranked list, not a complete recipe. The popular English "know your enemy and yourself and you cannot lose" upgrades the line into a promise the text does not make.
- **Design rule**: A readiness check has two halves, both filled in: 彼 (what the task and its environment actually are) and 己 (what each engine, tool and budget can actually do, marked reported versus verified). If either half is empty, treat the outcome as uncertain and do not plan as if it were assured.
- **Violated by**: Assigning a node to an engine because it is the default, without checking what that engine can do on this kind of task.
- **Sources**: claude-opus-5-5 (#3, RECALLED) and deepseek-v4-pro (#1, VERIFIED) carried it as a principle; minimax-m3.1-flash discussed the same line but demoted it to a caution against the maximal reading. The two that carried it agree; minimax's caution is correct and is folded into the reading above.

### 4. Intervene at the cheapest sufficient rung; the full assault is the admission that cheaper rungs failed
- **Text**: 故上兵伐謀，其次伐交，其次伐兵，其下攻城。攻城之法，為不得已。……是故百戰百勝，非善之善者也；不戰而屈人之兵，善之善者也。 — "The best general strikes at plans, next at alliances, next at armies, and the lowest besieges walled cities; siege is only when there is no alternative. … Winning a hundred battles out of a hundred is not the best of the best; subduing the enemy without fighting is." (謀攻篇, Attack by Stratagem)
- **Basis**: VERIFIED (source: Chinese Wikisource 孫子兵法, 謀攻第三 — fetched by minimax and deepseek)
- **Reading**: A ladder of targets ordered by cost, from the enemy's plans down to a walled city, with the explicit note that the last rung is done only when there is no alternative. 伐謀 is ambiguous — "attack the enemy's plans" (break his strategy) or "attack by means of planning" — but the ordering holds under either reading. 百戰百勝 and 不戰而屈人之兵 are the same cost argument seen from the winner's side, not a moral preference for peace: the best win is the one that cost no fighting.
- **Design rule**: Before scaling up, walk a fixed ladder and record why each cheaper rung was not enough: (a) remove the need (ask, reuse, find it already done), (b) one agent, (c) one agent plus an independent checker, (d) only last, a multi-agent graph. The last rung is legitimate only when labelled as the cheaper rungs having failed.
- **Violated by**: A third failed verification triggering "add four more reviewers and a longer loop" while the plan that was wrong goes unrepaired.
- **Sources**: all three — claude-opus-5-5 (#4, RECALLED), minimax-m3.1-flash (#2, VERIFIED), deepseek-v4-pro (#3, VERIFIED). Agreed.

### 5. Give the executor bounded authority to deviate; do not script its steps
- **Text**: 將能而君不御者勝。 — "A capable general whom the ruler does not restrain — that is victory." (謀攻篇, one of the five ways to know victory). With 塗有所不由，軍有所不擊，城有所不攻，地有所不爭，君命有所不受。 — "There are roads not to be taken, armies not to be struck, cities not to be assaulted, ground not to be contested, and the ruler's commands not to be obeyed." (九變篇, Variation in Tactics)
- **Basis**: VERIFIED (source: Chinese Wikisource 孫子兵法 — 將能而君不御 in 謀攻第三 and 君命有所不受 in 九變第八, both fetched by minimax; claude recalled the same lines)
- **Reading**: The authority rests on knowledge — the general can see the ground and the distant ruler cannot, and 謀攻篇 also lists how a ruler harms his army by ordering advance or retreat without knowing whether it can be done. The scope is bounded: "some commands," in a specific list, and the text gives no criterion for the refusal. It is not a licence to override the designer at will.
- **Design rule**: The orchestrator sets the objective, the bounds and the check, and does not script the executor's steps. An executor may depart from given steps when it observes they cannot work, but it must record the departure and the reason in the state file. Irreversible or outward-facing actions stay outside this authority (that limit is the designer's, not Sunzi's).
- **Violated by**: An orchestrator that dictates exact steps and treats any deviation as failure, even when the executor has seen that the step cannot work.
- **Sources**: claude-opus-5-5 (#11, RECALLED) and minimax-m3.1-flash (#12, VERIFIED, plus the 將能而君不御 line inside its #11); deepseek-v4-pro did not carry it. The two agree; deepseek's silence is a coverage gap, not a contradiction.

### 6. Secure the half you control; never rest the plan on hope
- **Text**: 昔之善戰者，先為不可勝，以待敵之可勝。不可勝在己，可勝在敵。……故曰：勝可知，而不可為。……勝兵先勝而後求戰，敗兵先戰而後求勝。 — "The skilled fighters of old first made themselves unbeatable, then waited for the enemy to become beatable. Being unbeatable lies with oneself; being beatable lies with the enemy. … So: victory can be known, but not made. … The army that wins makes its victory first and then seeks battle; the losing army seeks battle first and then seeks victory." (形篇, Dispositions). With 故用兵之法，無恃其不來，恃吾有以待之；無恃其不攻，恃吾有所不可攻也。 — "Do not rely on the enemy not coming; rely on being ready for him. Do not rely on his not attacking; rely on holding a position that cannot be attacked." (九變篇)
- **Basis**: VERIFIED (source: Chinese Wikisource 孫子兵法 — the 形篇 lines from 軍形第四, fetched by minimax and deepseek; the 九變篇 line from 九變第八, fetched by deepseek)
- **Reading**: The outcome is split in two: not losing is fully in your control; winning depends on the opponent's opening, which you cannot manufacture. 勝可知，而不可為 is genuinely ambiguous — "victory can be foreseen but not manufactured" or "one may know how to win without being able to do it" (minimax also records a variant 勝可擅, glossed 一作為) — and both readings point the same way. 勝兵先勝 belongs here, not with 廟算: the battle confirms an advantage you already built. The 九變篇 couplet is the same rule from the negative side: a plan may rest only on readiness, never on hope that the bad event will not occur.
- **Design rule**: Build the defensive invariants first (rollback, a read-only dry-run, budget caps, a check that can fail) so the worst outcome is "no progress," never "damage." Treat every premise of the form "X will not fail" as unsupported until it is replaced by "here is what happens when X fails." Do not treat the uncertain, winning half as something the design can guarantee.
- **Violated by**: A workflow that writes to shared state before it has a way to undo the write, or that carries an unhandled single point of failure it merely assumes will succeed.
- **Sources**: all three carry 先為不可勝 — claude-opus-5-5 (#2, RECALLED), minimax-m3.1-flash (#8, VERIFIED), deepseek-v4-pro (#4, VERIFIED); deepseek also carried 無恃其不來 as a separate principle (#11), which I merge here as the same idea. Agreed on the rule.

### 7. Get reliability from the configuration, not from demands on the individual
- **Text**: 故善戰者，求之於勢，不責於人，故能擇人而任勢。 — "The skilled fighter seeks it in the configuration (勢), and does not demand it of individuals; thus he can choose the right people and set them in the configuration." (勢篇, Energy)
- **Basis**: VERIFIED (source: Chinese Wikisource 孫子兵法, 兵勢第五 — fetched by minimax and deepseek)
- **Reading**: 勢 is the potential a situation's arrangement creates — the chapter illustrates it with logs and round stones rolled down a mountain: the general builds the slope, he does not urge each stone. 擇人 is ambiguous (the plain reading is "choose the right people and rely on 勢"; claude recalls, unchecked, a reading of 擇 as 釋, "let go of the individual and rely on 勢"); both readings put the mechanism in the structure. The chapter's 無智名，無勇功 adds that a good general's win looks unremarkable and is not credited to one brilliant action.
- **Design rule**: When a node keeps failing, change its structure first (inputs, check, contract, stop condition); only then ask whether the agent erred. Do not add exhortations to the prompt ("be careful", "double-check"). A result that depends on catching a strong model in the right slot is a design failure, not a success.
- **Violated by**: Answering a recurring error by appending "IMPORTANT: verify your work" instead of adding an independent check.
- **Sources**: all three — claude-opus-5-5 (#8, RECALLED), minimax-m3.1-flash (#7, VERIFIED), deepseek-v4-pro (#5, VERIFIED). Agreed.

### 8. Run the main line on the orthodox pattern; reserve a bounded share for the unorthodox
- **Text**: 凡戰者，以正合，以奇勝。 — "In battle, engage with the orthodox (正); win with the extraordinary (奇)." (勢篇, Energy). Claude also recalled, unverified, 奇正相生，如循環之無端 — "the extraordinary and the orthodox give rise to each other, like a ring without end."
- **Basis**: VERIFIED for 以正合，以奇勝 (source: Chinese Wikisource 孫子兵法, 兵勢第五 — fetched by deepseek). The 奇正相生 companion is RECALLED (claude only).
- **Reading**: 正 is the expected force that holds the engagement; 奇 is the unexpected one that decides it. 奇 is genuinely ambiguous — surprise, the unexpected, the non-obvious alternative — and the pair is held together, not opposed: the direct path fixes the engagement, the indirect path produces the win. Claude's recalled gloss (attributed to Cao Cao) takes 正 as whoever engages first and 奇 as whoever comes in later from the side, so the roles are positional rather than kinds of unit.
- **Design rule**: Give every workflow a boring, reliable default path that keeps things moving, and separately provision a small, capped share for a deliberate alternative, recording its results so a winning alternative can replace the default. Neither extreme — only the default, or everything-as-experiment — satisfies the rule.
- **Violated by**: A single-method pipeline with no alternative route, where one blocked step stalls the whole run; or its opposite, treating every run as an experiment so there is no stable baseline.
- **Sources**: claude-opus-5-5 (#7, RECALLED) and deepseek-v4-pro (#6, VERIFIED); minimax-m3.1-flash did not carry 正奇 (it chose 兵者，詭道也 instead, which I drop). The two that carried it agree.

### 9. Scale by fixed units and a fixed signalling protocol, not by headcount
- **Text**: 凡治眾如治寡，分數是也；鬥眾如鬥寡，形名是也。 — "Governing many is like governing few: it is a matter of divisions and counting (分數). Fighting with many is like fighting with few: it is a matter of forms and signals (形名)." (勢篇, Energy)
- **Basis**: RECALLED (claude only; not checked against any source)
- **Reading**: 分數 is organisation into units of fixed size under fixed commanders; 形名 is usually read as the signals — flags seen, gongs and drums heard — that let a large force move as one when the voice cannot carry. Some read 形名 more abstractly as "forms and their names," a defined vocabulary matching commands to formations. Under either reading a large force is controlled through structure and a shared code; the general does not talk to each soldier.
- **Design rule**: Before adding agents, define the unit structure (who reports to whom) and the message format (one state-file schema every node reads and writes). If the design cannot run two agents through that protocol, it will not run ten.
- **Violated by**: Fanning out to many subagents that each return free-form prose for the orchestrator to reconcile by hand.
- **Sources**: claude-opus-5-5 only (#6). No other note carried it; it stays RECALLED and single-source. Kept because the line is central to 勢篇 and the design need it answers is core to this project — but it is the weakest-evidence principle here, and its Chinese has not been checked.

### 10. Set the meeting ground; have the task arrive in your shape, not theirs
- **Text**: 故善戰者，致人而不致於人。 — "The skilled combatant moves the other and is not moved by him." (虛實篇, Weak Points and Strong). minimax also fetched the context: 凡先處戰地而待敵者佚，後處戰地而趨戰者勞 — "whoever first occupies the ground and waits is at ease; whoever hurries in later is exhausted."
- **Basis**: VERIFIED (source: Chinese Wikisource 孫子兵法, 虛實第六 — fetched by minimax and deepseek)
- **Reading**: 致 is ambiguous — "make (the enemy) come to me" or "converge on / move against (him)" — and deepseek adds the "impose or lure" split; both readings agree on who sets the agenda: the skilled party chooses the time and place rather than reacting. minimax's context line gives the concrete cost of the opposite case: arriving second, on the other's terms, is exhaustion.
- **Design rule**: The workflow sets its own sequence, checkpoints and stop conditions, and requires the task to arrive in a processable shape — the artifact, the failing test, the file — not a story. It does not let whichever event or output arrives first set the direction.
- **Violated by**: A coordinator that reacts to every incoming message in arrival order, so the loudest or earliest event sets the direction; or accepting a vaguely scoped request and spending the budget discovering what the deliverable is.
- **Sources**: minimax-m3.1-flash (#5, VERIFIED) and deepseek-v4-pro (#7, VERIFIED); claude-opus-5-5 did not carry it. The two agree on the rule and both flag the 致 ambiguity.

### 11. Guarding everything is guarding nothing; spend the check where it decides
- **Text**: 故備前則後寡，備後則前寡，備左則右寡，備右則左寡，無所不備，則無所不寡。 — "Guard the front and the rear is thin; guard the rear and the front is thin; guard the left and the right is thin; guard the right and the left is thin; guard everywhere and everywhere is thin." (虛實篇, Weak Points and Strong)
- **Basis**: VERIFIED (source: Chinese Wikisource 孫子兵法, 虛實第六 — fetched by deepseek; minimax quoted the same sentence inside its asymmetry principle)
- **Reading**: With fixed strength, covering more ground lowers the strength at each point. The chapter's complement (我專為一，敵分為十 — "I concentrate into one, the enemy divides into ten") names the positive form. The part that carries to design is the budget arithmetic, not the battlefield image.
- **Design rule**: Choose a small number of checks and spend real effort on each, ranked by consequence. Write down the risks deliberately left unchecked. A shallow check of every imaginable risk is the failure mode the line describes.
- **Violated by**: One reviewer asked to check security, performance, style, correctness and docs in a single pass with the budget that would have been spent on one of them.
- **Sources**: claude-opus-5-5 (#9, RECALLED) and deepseek-v4-pro (#8, VERIFIED); minimax-m3.1-flash cited the line as the mechanism inside its asymmetry principle (#4) rather than as a standalone. Agreed on the meaning.

### 12. The plan has no fixed form; re-plan from the observed state
- **Text**: 夫兵形象水，水之行，避高而趨下；兵之勝，避實而擊虛。水因地而制行，兵因敵而制勝。故兵無成勢，無恒形，能因敵變化而取勝者，謂之神。 — "An army's form is like water: water avoids the high and runs to the low; an army's victory avoids the solid and strikes the hollow. Water shapes its course to the ground; an army shapes its victory to the enemy. So an army has no fixed momentum and no constant form; one who wins by changing with the enemy is called divine." (虛實篇, Weak Points and Strong)
- **Basis**: VERIFIED (source: Chinese Wikisource 孫子兵法, 虛實第六 — fetched by minimax and deepseek, and the two agree on 水之行 / 兵之勝 / 無成勢 / 無恒形). Note: claude's recall gives the more familiar wording 水之形 / 兵之形 / 兵無常勢，水無常形, which differs at those points; I follow the fetched reading, and the meaning is unchanged either way.
- **Reading**: The army's form is set by the ground and the enemy, not fixed in advance; 避實擊虛 adds that effort goes where resistance is weakest. This does not say to abandon planning — the same text opens with 廟算 (see #1). It says the plan's form follows the observed conditions, and that a victory repeated identically (勝不復) is evidence the system has stopped adapting.
- **Design rule**: Fix the invariants — the check, the evidence, the stop condition — and leave the sequence free. At each checkpoint, the next step is chosen from the current state file, with an explicit option to re-plan. Effort goes to pieces the evidence shows are tractable; contested pieces are set aside, not attacked head-on.
- **Violated by**: A fixed linear script that runs step 5 even though step 3's output showed the assumption behind step 5 was false.
- **Sources**: all three — claude-opus-5-5 (#10, RECALLED), minimax-m3.1-flash (#6, VERIFIED), deepseek-v4-pro (#9, VERIFIED). Agreed on the rule; they differ only on the recalled versus fetched wording, noted above.

### 13. The circuitous route is the direct route
- **Text**: 軍爭之難者，以迂為直，以患為利。 — "The difficulty of manoeuvring lies in making the circuitous route direct, and turning misfortune into advantage." (軍爭篇, Maneuvering)
- **Basis**: VERIFIED (source: Chinese Wikisource 孫子兵法, 軍爭第七 — fetched by deepseek)
- **Reading**: 迂 (the roundabout way) and 直 (the straight way) are treated as convertible, not opposed: the longer path that avoids resistance arrives first, and a present hardship can be the means to an advantage. The claim is about total cost to a correct end, not apparent directness.
- **Design rule**: Prefer the path that looks longer but is reliable — the extra verification step, the explicit intermediate artifact, the checkpoint — over the shorter path that keeps hitting resistance and being redone. Count total time to a correct result, not apparent directness.
- **Violated by**: Skipping the intermediate check "to save time," then spending more total time on rework than the check would have cost.
- **Sources**: deepseek-v4-pro only (#10). Single-source but VERIFIED; kept because the line is in the received text and the design rule is clean.

### 14. Act only on named advantage, weigh the harm alongside, and stop when the gain stops
- **Text**: 合於利而動，不合於利而止。 — "Move when it accords with advantage; stop when it does not." (火攻篇, repeated in 九地篇). With 非利不動，非得不用，非危不戰。主不可以怒而興師，將不可以慍而致戰。 — "Do not move without advantage, do not employ troops without gain, do not fight unless at risk. A ruler must not raise an army in anger, nor a general fight in resentment." (火攻篇). Claude also recalled, unverified, 是故智者之慮，必雜於利害 — "the wise person's deliberation always mixes advantage and harm" (九變篇) and 故不盡知用兵之害者，則不能盡知用兵之利也 — "one who does not fully know the harm of using troops cannot fully know its benefit" (作戰篇).
- **Basis**: VERIFIED for 合於利而動 / 非利不動 / 主不可以怒 (source: Chinese Wikisource 孫子兵法, 火攻第十二 — fetched by minimax and deepseek). The 雜於利害 and 不盡知用兵之害 lines are RECALLED (claude only).
- **Reading**: Advantage is the only licence to act or to keep acting, and the same test applies to starting and to stopping. The argument for restraint is irreversibility — anger can turn back to joy, but a destroyed state cannot be restored. The recalled companion lines add the other half: benefit and harm are known together or not at all, so a plan that names its payoff but not its failure mode has not actually weighed anything.
- **Design rule**: Every node names the state it changes, the check that will see the change, and its specific failure mode, side by side. Every loop has an objective stop condition ("we are no longer gaining"), and stopping is decided by that condition, not by sunk cost, impatience or frustration. A node that names no failure mode, or whose check can pass either way, is not ready.
- **Violated by**: A retry loop whose only exit is "a human got tired" or "the budget ran out"; or a design document that lists what each stage produces and nothing about how each stage could fail silently.
- **Sources**: all three carry fragments of this one idea — claude-opus-5-5 (#12, RECALLED, via 雜於利害), minimax-m3.1-flash (#9, VERIFIED, via 非利不動), deepseek-v4-pro (#12, VERIFIED, via 主不可以怒 / 合於利而動). They emphasise different halves but agree the gate is advantage, not emotion or habit.

### 15. Foreknowledge must come from someone who actually looked, not from divination, analogy or computation
- **Text**: 先知者，不可取於鬼神，不可象於事，不可驗於度，必取於人，知敵之情者也。 — "Foreknowledge cannot be got from ghosts and spirits, cannot be got by analogy with past events, cannot be verified by reckoning (度); it must be got from people who know the enemy's situation." (用間篇, Use of Spies)
- **Basis**: VERIFIED (source: Chinese Wikisource 孫子兵法, 用間第十三 — fetched by minimax and deepseek; their transcriptions of the line agree)
- **Reading**: Three inadmissible sources are ruled out one by one — the supernatural, analogy to precedent, and a priori reckoning — in favour of a single admissible source: a person in contact with the actual situation. 度 is traditionally glossed as astronomical or calendrical reckoning (度數), so the line rejects divination-and-computation as a class; it should not be read as a distrust of empirical measurement. The chapter then lists five kinds of spies and the turning of the enemy's spies, which implies many sources checked against each other.
- **Design rule**: A claim about current state (a file's contents, a tool's behaviour, an API's availability) counts only if a node actually read or ran it in this run, with the evidence attached. A claim inferred from how similar systems usually behave is marked unverified; where it matters it needs a second, independent source.
- **Violated by**: A planning node asserting what a function does from its name and the usual convention, without opening the file.
- **Sources**: all three — claude-opus-5-5 (#13, RECALLED), minimax-m3.1-flash (#3, VERIFIED), deepseek-v4-pro (#13, VERIFIED). Agreed.

## What I dropped, and why

1. **兵者，詭道也 / 形人而我無形 ("war is the way of deception; show a shape, keep the interior") — minimax #4.** Dropped. The line is real and central (計篇), but it describes deceiving an adversary, and all three notes' "what I would not claim" sections agree it does not transfer to a cooperative agent team. A workflow that hides its state from its own verifier is abusing the line, not obeying it — minimax itself flagged this as "Loose." Deception toward an enemy is not a design rule for agents that must trust each other's record.

2. **朝氣銳，晝氣惰，暮氣歸 (morale decays; "put the sharp unit where agreement is automatic") — minimax #10.** Dropped. Single source, and the note's transcription of the passage is corrupted (the quote breaks mid-line into English), so I cannot cite it cleanly. The residual insight — a long run wears down the later nodes — is already carried by #2 (bound the duration); the specific mapping to "where agreement is automatic" is strained.

3. **犯之以事，勿告以言 / 率然 ("commit them by the task and do not tell them the reasoning"; coordinate like one body) — minimax #13.** Dropped the sharp half. "Do not tell your agents the reasoning" is a hard reading that minimax itself flags as loose and warns leads to stripping the context agents need. The coordination substance — integration lives in the interface, not the briefing — is kept under #9 (分數形名); the maxim itself is dropped.

4. **道者，令民與上同意 / 上下同欲者勝 (shared purpose is a precondition) — minimax #11.** Dropped. Single source. The line is real (謀攻篇's five ways to know victory), but its load is already split across principles I keep: the "know your own side" half is #3, and the "capable general not restrained by the ruler" half is #5. The residue — "write the objective once so every prompt traces to it" — is sound practice but not a distinct classical principle beyond what is kept.

5. **六敗 (地形篇: 走、弛、陷、崩、亂、北, and the 卒強吏弱 / 吏強卒弱 strength mismatches) — claude #14.** Dropped. Single source and RECALLED, never checked against a text. Its design rule — a post-mortem blames the design choice before it blames the model — is already carried by #7 (求之於勢，不責於人). The strength-matching gloss (a weak verifier cannot hold a strong worker; a strong controller pushes weak workers past their limits) is useful but recalled-only, so I note it here rather than canonising it.

## What this domain cannot supply

1. **A rule for intra-team trust and honest information sharing.** The text's model is adversary-against-adversary; its deception doctrine (兵者，詭道也) points the wrong way for a cooperative team. 孫子兵法 tells you how to fool an enemy and how to command a force of soldiers — but nothing about how autonomous agents should share state truthfully or align incentives. Expecting it to answer "how do I keep my own agents honest" reads past what the text is about.

2. **A guarantee of the correct answer.** 勝可知，而不可為 ("victory can be known, but not made") is an explicit refusal to promise a manufactured win. The text lets you guarantee the defensive half — no damage, evidence gathered, the check ran, the loop stopped — but the winning half depends on what it calls "the enemy," the part you do not control. A workflow whose contract is "produce the correct answer" is making a promise the source text says cannot be made.

3. **A concrete org chart, message format, or decision protocol.** The text insists structure matters (分數形名, 求之於勢) but gives no division of labour, no schema, no handoff rule. It is a text of principles, not a spec; every concrete design detail — who reports to whom, what the state file looks like, which actions need approval — must be supplied by the designer. Treating 孫子兵法 as a source of workflow mechanics is a category confusion.
