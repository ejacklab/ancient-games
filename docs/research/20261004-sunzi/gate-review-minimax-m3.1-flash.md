## Verdict

the bookkeeping half of this text: what must be counted, written down and proved before anything is committed. On

### 1.1. G3 counts field names, not what is in them

- **Principle**: 「先知者，不可取於鬼神，不可象於事，不可驗於度，必取於人，知敵之情者也。」 — "Foreknowledge cannot be obtained from spirits or gods, cannot be obtained by analogy with past events, cannot be obtained by measurement or calculation; it must be obtained from people — those who know the enemy's situation." **Basis: VERIFIED** (Chinese Wikisource 孫子兵法, fetched 2026-10-05) — `deepseek-v4-pro.md` §13, whose design rule is that a claim not traceable to something actually observed must be *labelled as such and treated as untrusted input*.
- **Why it applies**: `FIVE - {str(x).lower() for x in n.get("five_things", [])}` at `design_gate.py:146` asks whether the five strings `tools/context/contract/evidence/state` are present. Those five things *are* the node's claims about what it is given and what it will hand back, so the check settles that the field exists and never that anything is in it — `state: "the run journal, read before every step"` and `state: "state"` are the same to line 146, and the 300 designs took the second path. 先知 is a rule about the *provenance* of a claim, and its second half is the one the gate needs: an unbacked claim is admissible if it is marked unbacked. The gate allows unmarked claims and cannot tell a marked one from a bare one, because the schema has no place to write the mark.
- **The change**: `five_things` stops being a list of names and becomes an object keyed by the same five names (the schema block at `docs/TASK_TYPES.md:284` grows with it — the gate cannot check a key the format has no slot for). G3 gains **G3b**: every one of the five keys must be present with a value that is a string, is not equal to its own key, and is not a document pointer; `?` is the explicit *unestablished* marker, accepted, and rejected with a finding on a building node's `context` or `contract`, which method 3.6 does require. Findings must distinguish "declares nothing" from "declares X", because a reader will otherwise assume the gate verified the content — and it did not; it checked the shape of a claim, which is all §13's design rule asks of a script.
- **Falsified by**: A node that genuinely has nothing to put in one of the five — a single-node read-only scan whose `tools` is one shell call — where forcing non-placeholder text makes the designer write filler that is no more true than the name, and the new finding blocks an honest design.

### 1.2. No rule for a baseline

- **Principle**: 「昔之善戰者，先為不可勝，以待敵之可勝。不可勝在己，可勝在敵。故善戰者，能為不可勝，不能使敵之必可勝。故曰：勝可知，而不可為。」 — "The skilled general first made himself unbeatable and waited for the enemy to become beatable. The unbeatable lies with oneself; the beatable lies with the enemy. … Victory can be known; it cannot be manufactured." **Basis: VERIFIED** (Wikisource, fetched 2026-10-05) — `minimax-m3.1-flash.md` §8, whose design rule is to split every obligation into a part you can guarantee and a part you can only observe, and treat the guaranteed half as what the artifact delivers.
- **Why it applies**: Method 3.6 makes the recorded baseline one of three objective parts of every building node's stop, and the string `baseline` appears nowhere in the 365 lines. This is exactly the split 軍形 draws, and the gate is on the wrong side of it: whether the product was already correct is the half nobody can argue about afterwards — it either sits in the state file or it does not — while whether the change is right belongs to the reviewer. A gate that lets a design skip the recording has guaranteed nothing; it has merely not been shown to have lost, and 無算 counts as a loss in this text, not a pass.
- **The change**: new **G9**. The design JSON gains a top-level `baseline: {"command": str, "output": str, "exit_code": int, "recorded_at": str}`. G9 fires when any node builds and `baseline` is absent, when `output` is empty or `"—"`, when `exit_code` is absent, and when a building node's `stop` does not cite the baseline command. State plainly in the finding text what G9 does *not* do: it must not require `exit_code == 0`. A red baseline is a fact to be recorded and routed, and 不可勝在己 is the half you own — the record exists — not the half you would like to own, which is the record's contents.
- **Falsified by**: The greenfield case at `docs/TASK_TYPES.md:270`, where the target is new and no test command exists at all, G9 blocks a run the method intends to allow, and the design is pushed to invent a baseline, which is worse than none.

### 1.3. No rule for the three-part stop

- **Principle**: 「非利不動，非得不用，非危不戰。主不可以怒而興師，將不可以慍而致戰。…合於利而動，不合於利而止。」 — "Do not move without advantage; do not employ troops without obtaining; do not fight unless at risk. … Move in accordance with advantage; halt when there is none." **Basis: VERIFIED** (Wikisource, fetched 2026-10-05) — `minimax-m3.1-flash.md` §9, whose design rule ends: "A node whose check can pass either way is not a node, it is a ritual."
- **Why it applies**: The gate never reads `stop`. Line 153 asks only that `loop.exit` be a non-empty string, so `"fresh node on stronger tier"` and `"looks fine"` are identical to it, and a node carrying no loop is asked for no stop at all (1.6). 合於利而止 makes stopping a first-class named action with a condition attached, and the algorithm names three objective parts and enforces none of them. A stop that can pass either way is the 儀式 case exactly: the node that costs a slot, returns a summary, and decides nothing — which is what a design writing "reviewed" as its stop has built.
- **The change**: G3 gains **G3c**. For every node whose category is product `yes`, or `mixed` under `builds: true`, require `stop: {"criteria": [ids], "baseline": true | str, "must_not_change": [globs]}`; fire on a missing key, an empty list, or a criteria id not of the form `R<n>.<n>` / `N<n>.<n>`. The schema in `docs/TASK_TYPES.md` must grow the field first. Declare the human-acceptance exemption in the node rather than in the code's private list — `stop.criteria: "EJ acceptance"` for the `ui/ux dev` row, whose check cell is half human — so a reader can see which stop was waived and why.
- **Falsified by**: A building node whose work is served by no criterion at all — a fixture regenerated in `test data gen` — where requiring a criterion id makes the designer cite one the piece does not serve, and a false citation is a worse artefact than the missing rule it was added to prevent.

### 1.4 (and 2.6). The table's own sabotage column is parsed, discarded, and never exercised

- **Principle**: 「故用兵之法，無恃其不來，恃吾有以待之；無恃其不攻，恃吾有所不可攻也。」 — "In war, do not rely on the enemy not coming; rely on being ready for him. Do not rely on the enemy not attacking; rely on holding a position that cannot be attacked." **Basis: VERIFIED** (Wikisource, fetched 2026-10-05) — `deepseek-v4-pro.md` §11, whose design rule is that every premise of the form "X will not fail" must be replaced by "here is what happens when X fails".
- **Why it applies**: The sixth column of `docs/TASK_TYPES.md:86` is headed *Sabotage (proof the check can fail)* — the document states the principle the gate is silent about, and `design_gate.py:93` parses it into `cats[name]["sabotage"]`, where it is never read. Every check the gate accepts today is accepted on the design's word that it would go red, which is 無恃其不來 with the fields filled in: the gate relies on the absence of a failure it has never invited. 2.6 is the same hole from the other side — `corpus_check.py:63` builds the designs out of the same cells `parse_types` produces, so even a working sabotage rule would be satisfied by copying the cell it was supposed to test.
- **The change**: G3 gains **G3d**. Every node carries `sabotage: {"break": str, "expect": "check goes red", "status": "unrun" | "ran-red"}`; fire when `status` is `unrun` on a building node, and when `break` is empty or a bare pointer. Cross-check `break` against the table's sabotage cell for the node's category so a copy of the cell shows up in the finding: the cell is the *proposal*, the node's status is the *evidence*, and treating the proposal as the evidence is what made the column look already-satisfied. Skip the rule when the cell is `—`. On 2.6's side, `corpus_check.py` must stop being the only constructor: the sabotage-bearing path needs designs that came from somewhere else before G3d's verdicts mean anything.
- **Falsified by**: The `others` row, whose pattern, check and sabotage cells are all `—` and which method 3.2 sends every unmatched piece to, where a rule demanding a sabotage of every node blocks the algorithm's own catch-all and the blockage is routed around with an invented one.

### 1.5. G2 reads the facts it is checking from the design it is checking

- **Principle**: 「凡先處戰地而待敵者佚，後處戰地而趨戰者勞。故善戰者，致人而不致於人。」 — "Whoever first occupies the ground and waits for the enemy is at ease; whoever comes later and hurries to the fight is exhausted. Hence the skilled general brings the enemy to him and is not brought to him." **Basis: VERIFIED** (Wikisource, fetched 2026-10-05) — `minimax-m3.1-flash.md` §5, whose design rule is to require the task to arrive in a shape the workflow can process — the artefact, the file, the failing test, not a story.
- **Why it applies**: Lines 130-131 take `builds` and `touched_paths` from the design under examination, so G2 can only fire on a design that contradicts itself — and a self-report contradicting itself is the one thing a self-report cannot do. A design claiming `research and reports`, `builds: false`, `touched_paths: []` while quietly rewriting product code is internally consistent and passes; there is nothing in the file to notice. 致人而不致於人 is the situation inverted: the gate is being *moved* by the account handed to it, and the ground facts arrive as a story about the ground. What is at stake is not tidiness — it is that the one rule in the gate about *the design matching reality* is the one rule with no contact with reality.
- **The change**: the design stops asserting the facts and points at them. `builds` and `touched_paths` are replaced by `facts: {"cmd": "git status --porcelain", "output": str, "at": iso8601}`. G2 recomputes `builds` as "the recorded output contains a path under the product root" and `touched` as the listed paths; fire when the design's declared `builds` disagrees with the recomputed value, and when a node's category is product `yes` while the recorded facts show no product path. The designer may not supply the value; only the command's output may — and the finding must quote the line of output it is relying on, so the designer can argue with the evidence rather than with the rule.
- **Falsified by**: The `tiny` exit, where a one-sentence session-control directive legitimately touches nothing and an empty porcelain is the only evidence there will ever be, so a ground-read G2 has no ground to read and rejects a design the method blesses.

### 1.6. The loop rules run only where a loop already is

- **Principle**: 「故兵聞拙速，未睹巧之久也。夫兵久而國利者，未之有也。」 with 「故兵貴勝，不貴久。」 — "In war one hears of clumsy speed; one has not seen cleverness that lasts long. There has never been a prolonged war that benefited the state. … In war, value victory, not duration." **Basis: RECALLED (from training)** — `claude-opus-5-5.md` §5, which I have not checked against an edition. Its design rule: every loop gets a hard cap and a stop condition set before it starts, and "if a node has no cap, the design is not finished."
- **Why it applies**: `if lp is not None:` at line 150 is the gate's entire loop policy — no key, no rules — so the condition that would need a cap is the condition that opts out of needing one. The gate's own author knew this shape of trap: G5 at line 171 explicitly excludes `bool` from its number test, and G3's `isinstance(lp.get("limit"), int)` does not. I ran it: `{"limit": true, "exit": "x", "feedback": "y"}` produces zero findings, because `isinstance(True, int)` is True in Python and `True < 1` is False. So the cap is a key the design may omit, and when it is present it need not be a number — a limit that is not a limit and a loop that is not required are the same defect at two depths.
- **The change**: split the condition in two. G3 requires a `loop` on every node whose category's `pattern` cell names a loop (`evaluator–optimizer`, `codegen loop`, `loop with the failing test`), and requires `loop: null` together with `no_loop_reason` quoting the pattern cell verbatim on every node it does not require one for — which puts the previously inert `pattern` column to work as the discriminator. Independently, tighten line 151 to `type(lp.get("limit")) is int and lp["limit"] >= 1`, the same bool exclusion G5 already uses, and reject a `limit` above a per-category maximum so a design cannot buy ten thousand attempts by writing the number.
- **Falsified by**: A category whose pattern cell names no loop but whose work genuinely iterates — `test cases gen` is listed as "single node + review" and a mutant hunt is a loop — where a pattern-keyed requirement leaves the iteration optional and the design states it nowhere, and the finding that would have caught it is not the finding this change adds.

### 1.7. The check only has to be non-empty

- **Principle**: 「昔之善戰者，先為不可勝，以待敵之可勝。…故曰：勝可知，而不可為。」 — "The skilled general first made himself unbeatable and waited. … Victory can be known; it cannot be manufactured." **Basis: RECALLED (from training)** — `claude-opus-5-5.md` §2. That note puts, in the half you own, a list of guard-rails against loss — rollback, a dry-run, budget caps, **a check that can fail** — and this is the entry in it.
- **Why it applies**: Line 144 asks `if not (n.get("check") or "").strip()`. The 300 designs pass on `"default check for code generation (TASK_TYPES.md)"`, which is a pointer to a document rather than a check, and which contains the word "check" — so the string satisfies the rule that is supposed to be about checks. The text puts a check that can fail on the side you own because it is the cheapest member of that list and the one most often quietly replaced by an assurance: `"the check"` is an assurance, `pytest -q green` is a thing that returns non-zero, and the gate cannot currently tell them apart. It therefore certifies the first while believing it has taken the second, which is the whole of 1.1 and 1.7 in one line of code.
- **The change**: G3's check test becomes structural rather than existential. Require the node's `check` to contain the table's check cell for its category verbatim or as a prefix, so paraphrasing a check into a pointer is a finding; and require it to name something executable or enumerable — a command (a program token plus arguments), an inline enumerated checklist of three or more items, or a named artefact with its pass condition. A check consisting only of a document reference fires with the message "check is a pointer to {path}, not a check". Declare the human exemption from the table's own check cells that name a person, so `ui/ux dev`'s "build/render script + EJ's acceptance" is legal by construction and the exemption is visible in the code instead of hidden in someone's judgement.
- **Falsified by**: That same `ui/ux dev` cell, whose check is half human, because any exemption broad enough to admit a human step admits a pointer too — and if the only way to keep the rule is to make it exempt, `p050.json` passes again and the finding is cosmetic.

### 2.1. The designs are built from the cells the gate parses

- **Principle**: 「道者，令民與上同意，可與之死，可與之生，而不畏危也。…故知勝有五：…上下同欲者勝。」 — "The Way is that the people below agree with the commander above, so that you can go with them to die and go with them to live, and they are not afraid of danger. … There are five ways of knowing victory: … upper and lower sharing one desire." **Basis: VERIFIED** (Wikisource, fetched 2026-10-05) — `minimax-m3.1-flash.md` §11, which reads 同意 as measured under load precisely because 可與之死 is the extreme case in the sentence.
- **Why it applies**: `tests/workflows/corpus_check.py:63` reads the same `types` dict that `parse_types` produces and writes the check as an f-string over the category name, so agreement between design and gate is true by construction before the gate is called. 上下同欲 is agreement measured at 可與之死, not agreement of restatement, and Run 1 measured restatement: the two sides were never asked to disagree, so they could not. A zero-bug result from that arrangement is not evidence about the 300 designs; it is evidence about the f-string, and the log's own sentence — "Every bug here would be the algorithm's own fault" — is true only of a bug the f-string was capable of committing.
- **The change**: the corpus needs a second construction path that does not read `types`. Emit a fixed share (30 of 300 is enough) from hand-written design files under `runs/20261004-corpus-fulltest/designs/` with checks, engines, loops and stops written by a person, and report the two populations' finding counts separately rather than as one number. A rule's verdict is only measured on designs that were not built to satisfy it.
- **Falsified by**: Diffing the hand-built designs against the generated ones and finding they differ only in whitespace, ordering and category spelling, in which case the circularity is present but not load-bearing and the honest fix is a sentence in the log, not a second path.

### 2.2. Every design was a default, so the challenger rules never executed

- **Principle**: 「凡戰者，以正合，以奇勝。」 with 「奇正相生，如循環之無端，孰能窮之？」 — "In battle, engage with the direct; win with the indirect. The extraordinary and the orthodox give rise to each other, like a ring without end; who can exhaust them?" **Basis: RECALLED (from training)** — `claude-opus-5-5.md` §7. That note is careful in a way I will not paper over: the project's 20% challenger rule "has the same shape, which is a parallel and not evidence for it". I use the parallel as a parallel.
- **Why it applies**: `build_design` hardcodes `"design_source": "default"`, so G5's five sub-rules and G7's challenger branch were never entered once across 300 designs — together, something near a third of the gate's rule surface. 正 is the entire force and 奇 never appeared, so 奇正相生 could not run: a challenger that has produced no verdict can never become the new 正, and the run ends with no measurement of the one property that makes the deviation worth budgeting — whether the gate can price it.
- **The change**: the corpus must split the way the algorithm tells runs to split. Emit roughly 20% `design_source: {"type": "challenger"}` designs carrying real `estimate` / `default_estimate` token counts, spread deliberately: claims of 0.19 and 0.20 to sit on the boundary, one with a correct margin and unequal coverage, one whose `basis` is whitespace. Report G5 and G7 findings per class, not in aggregate. G7 needs its own fixtures: `validate_ledger_row` is reachable today only from `main`, so give `--self-test` a ledger row per branch and let the corpus call the same helper.
- **Falsified by**: Running challenger designs and finding G5 rejects one the method considers correct — a genuine 21% margin whose cost is dominated by fixed overhead the token ratio cannot see — in which case what the run has established is that the arithmetic is wrong, not that the gate was untested, and the fix belongs in G5's denominator.

### 2.3. No negative controls

- **Principle**: 「知彼知己者，百戰不殆；不知彼而知己，一勝一負；不知彼不知己，每戰必殆。」 — "Know the other and know yourself, and in a hundred battles you will not be imperilled; not knowing the other but knowing yourself, one win, one loss; knowing neither, every battle is peril." **Basis: RECALLED (from training)** — `claude-opus-5-5.md` §3, whose design rule requires each half to be "measured or at least marked *reported* versus *verified*".
- **Why it applies**: Line 333 prints `self-test: PASS — the gate can fail`, and `expect()` at line 281 is called nineteen times with designs derived from `_good_design()`. Some calls assert a finding appears and some assert the design is clean, but no field is tested in both directions: the script's sentence about itself is a *report*, and the note's own reported-versus-verified distinction is the one `design_gate.py` never makes about itself. 一勝一負 is exactly what knowing your own side alone buys — 300 self-played games is a record of yourself, and it says nothing about how the other side plays or whether the referee can call a foul.
- **The change**: turn `self_test` into a mutation matrix, and let the matrix fail. For every field the method requires — the five things, `check`, `stop`, `baseline`, `loop.limit` / `exit` / `feedback`, `criteria`, `sabotage`, `builds`, `touched_paths`, `design_source`, `coverage` — mutate exactly that field of `_good_design()` to a known-bad value and assert the expected G-rule fires; then mutate it to a *second* known-bad value and assert again, because one bad value per field only proves the field is read and not that it is read for the right reason. Keep the existing converse assertion: the unmutated design must yield zero findings. `--self-test` exits non-zero on any missed mutation, and the summary line prints `mutations: N, caught: M, missed: K` so a loss of coverage is visible in the output rather than discoverable only by reading the source.
- **Falsified by**: A mutation the matrix cannot catch that turns out to be a field the method does not require — `estimate.tokens` on a default design, for instance — in which case the miss is not a defect and any "mutations caught" percentage derived from the matrix is a number with no denominator, which is 2.5's defect in new clothes.

### 2.4. The second layer was declared and never staffed

- **Principle**: 「故兵有走者、有弛者、有陷者、有崩者、有亂者、有北者。凡此六者，非天之災，將之過也。…將弱不嚴，教道不明，吏卒無常，陳兵縱橫，曰亂。」 — "An army may flee, be slack, sink, collapse, fall into chaos, or be routed. These six are not disasters from Heaven; they are the general's faults. … A weak, lax general, unclear instruction, officers and men without fixed roles, troops drawn up every which way: chaos." **Basis: RECALLED (from training)** — `claude-opus-5-5.md` §14, whose design rule is that a post-mortem first asks which design choice allowed the failure, and that every node has a fixed role.
- **Why it applies**: `docs/TASK_TYPES.md:283` names the class the script cannot see and states that the checklist layer exists for exactly it, and the gate's own docstring names the layer and its engine. So the layer had a declared role in the design's structure, and no node in any of the 300 runs held it. That is 吏卒無常 — officers and men without fixed roles — and the text's verdict is 亂, charged to the commander and explicitly not to Heaven. A run that reports a two-layer result having staffed one layer is the failure the chapter is describing: not a weak unit, a missing one.
- **The change**: make the unstaffed layer a hole in a record rather than a sentence in a docstring. Add a `checklist_reviewed` column to `LEDGER_KEYS` so the ledger requires it non-empty for every row, and add **G10**: a design whose node checks contain a judgement term drawn from the table's own check cells (acceptance, review, judged, rubric) must carry a `code review` node whose `check` names an enumerated fixed checklist. G10 is the mechanical residue of what `docs/TASK_TYPES.md:283` hands to a human, and it converts the layer's absence from silence into a finding.
- **Falsified by**: A run in which the checklist reviewer turns out to be a second pass of the same gate over the same 300 designs, because then the second layer adds no evidence the mutation run does not already supply, the ledger column records an activity rather than a result, and G10 buys a finding nobody needs.

### 2.5. The reported accuracies had no denominator

- **Principle**: 「夫未戰而廟算勝者，得算多也；未戰而廟算不勝者，得算少也。多算勝，少算不勝，而況於無算乎？」 — "The side that wins the counting in the temple before battle has many counts; who loses has few. Many counts win, few do not — how much less, none at all." **Basis: VERIFIED** (Wikisource, fetched 2026-10-05) — `minimax-m3.1-flash.md` §1, which reads 得算多 as either "more factors scored in our favour" or "calculated more thoroughly" and holds the claim to be quantitative and comparative either way.
- **Why it applies**: `runs/20261004-corpus-fulltest/testlog.md:33` reports accuracy "measured separately on the two clean rounds", and r1 — 25 of 25 mismatched, so by that definition not clean — sits outside the denominator of every published figure, without the sentence saying what the 90% now divides. A count whose membership is settled after the count is not a smaller count; it is a different count, and 少算 is the text's word for a reckoning that is real and thin. The 73/150 in the same table is a third quantity, and the three figures are printed as if one scale.
- **The change**: `check_round` already returns its classes (`MISMATCH`, `GAP`, `BUILDER`, `GATE`), so the summary at `corpus_check.py:207` must group by class and print `classified`, `skipped` and per-round `matched/total` before any percentage, and the log must carry the exclusion on the same line as the figure. No aggregate of a subset without its exclusion. This is a harness change, not a gate change, and it is the cheapest one in this review: it alters no number, only the honesty of the ones already printed.
- **Falsified by**: r1's 25/25 turning out to be a results-file coverage artefact of the same kind as the `no result row` rows — a missing file rather than a wrong classifier — in which case excluding it was honest reporting, the denominator was never narrowed, and my finding is a disagreement about presentation rather than about the number.

## Where the analogy breaks

**1. There is no adversary, and every line in these notes is about one.** 致人而不致於人, 上兵伐謀, 以正合以奇勝, 無恃其不來 — all of them presuppose a second party with its own will, which conceals, which feeds, and which may be lying. `design_gate.py` faces a JSON file. That file cannot adapt, cannot feed, cannot deceive, and cannot be defeated; it can only be malformed. So the strategic content of this body of thought — information asymmetry, commitment, deterrence, the timing of a decisive blow — has no referent in the thing under review. What transfers is the *administrative* half: what must be counted, recorded and proven before commitment, and the discipline of never resting a plan on the hope that a bad event will not occur. That is a real transfer, but it is a fraction of the original claim, and any sentence of mine that sounds as if I am importing 以正合以奇勝 as evidence about a JSON validator is overstating what survived. I declined to use minimax §4 (形人而我無形) for exactly this reason — that note itself warns that mapping it inward is "abusing the line rather than obeying it" — and I declined claude-opus's 兵者，詭道也 for the same reason, which both notes independently refuse.

**2. The text is about victory; the family here is about truth-detection.** 兵貴勝，不貴久 values victory over duration; 百戰百勝，非善之善者也 devalues even a perfect battle record; 攻城 is the last resort because it is expensive. All of that is a theory of *acquiring* an outcome cheaply. A gate's job is not to win — it is to be right about a design that will be run later, by someone else, on a different day, and its natural failure is not losing a campaign but endorsing a false promise and then being cited as having endorsed it. This book has no vocabulary for a mechanism rewarded for saying no, and 勝可知而不可為 is a caution against manufacturing an outcome, not a theory of the referee who refuses to certify one. The nearest honest statement in these notes is the second half of the graded scale in minimax's closing section — 「不知彼而知己，一勝一負」 — and even that is about the general, not about the instrument that judges him.

**3. 備前則後寡 does not bind on a validator, so the obvious advice from it is wrong here.** "Guard the front and the rear is thin; prepare everywhere and everywhere is thin" (**Basis: VERIFIED**, Wikisource, fetched 2026-10-05; `minimax-m3.1-flash.md` §4's Reading, `deepseek-v4-pro.md` §8) is a real principle about a fixed budget placed against a fixed front. A JSON validator's marginal cost per rule is effectively zero, and its scarce resource is not compute but the designer's attention and the reviewer's reading time. So the concentration argument — choose a few checks and spend on them — would lead to the *opposite* of the right instruction for this file: a script should check everything it can cheaply check, and the discipline belongs on the human layers instead. That is why none of the twelve blocks above is argued from 備前則後寡, and I would resist a future recommendation that is.

## The one change

Make `--self-test` a mutation matrix (2.3): for every field method 3.6 requires, mutate that field to two known-bad values and assert the expected G-rule fires, plus the existing converse that the unmutated design is clean, and exit non-zero on any miss. It costs no model calls, it is the only one of the thirteen that measures the other twelve, and until it exists every claim I have made above — including that `limit: true` and `sabotage: "TODO"` pass — is a code reading rather than a test result, which is the condition 2.5 is about.
