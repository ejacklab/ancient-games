# 孫子兵法 — minimax-m3.1-flash

Source of every `VERIFIED` line below: the received Chinese text of the thirteen chapters at Chinese Wikisource,
`zh.wikisource.org/wiki/孫子兵法`, fetched 2026-10-05. That page is a received edition with editorial apparatus — it
carries 校勘 notes, variant readings, and a `答話` appendix — not a critical edition, and I could not check it against
any other witness. Every English rendering is my own translation of that text, not a quotation from a named
translator. I hold no source in front of me other than that fetch, so nothing here is `RECALLED`; where I could not
verify a wording, I say so inside the principle.

### 1. Design first, and let the plan's arithmetic outrank the executor's cleverness
- **Text**: 兵者，國之大事，死生之地，存亡之道，不可不察也。…夫未戰而廟算勝者，得算多也；未戰而廟算不勝者，得算少也。多算勝，少算不勝，而況於無算乎？ — "War is the great affair of state, the ground of life and death, the way of survival and ruin; it must not go unexamined. … He who wins the counting in the temple before the battle has many counts; who loses has few. Many counts win, few do not — how much less, none at all." (始計第一)
- **Basis**: VERIFIED (source: Wikisource 孫子兵法, fetched 2026-10-05)
- **Reading**: The chapter names five things to examine (道 天 地 將 法) and seven comparisons to compute, then says the outcome is readable off the count. 廟算 is arithmetic done in the ancestral shrine before the army moves, so the claim is that the number of things settled in advance is itself a predictor. The examination is not paperwork; it is the instrument.
- **Design rule**: A workflow does not begin by dispatching; it begins by writing the plan down and scoring it against named criteria — how many nodes, which checks, what can go wrong, what stops it — before any node runs. A plan that cannot be scored is not started.
- **Violated by**: The designer opens with "spawn three agents and see what comes back", states no objective, no success condition and no stop condition, and then treats the first output as evidence about the problem.

### 2. Escalate from the cheapest intervention; the last rung is doing the work
- **Text**: 故上兵伐謀，其次伐交，其次伐兵，其下攻城。攻城之法，為不得已。…故善用兵者，屈人之兵而非戰也，拔人之城而非攻也，毀人之國而非久也，必以全爭於天下…是故百戰百勝，非善之善者也；不戰而屈人之兵，善之善者也。 — "The best general strikes at the enemy's plans; the next at his alliances; the next at his army. Assaulting a city is the last resort, undertaken only because there is no alternative. … The skilled general breaks the enemy without a battle, takes the city without storming it, destroys the state without a long siege, and so wins the whole world without leaving the enemy broken. … Therefore to win a hundred battles out of a hundred is not the best of the good; to break the enemy without a battle is." (謀攻第三)
- **Basis**: VERIFIED (source: Wikisource 孫子兵法, fetched 2026-10-05)
- **Reading**: A four-rung ladder ordered by cost, with the rule that force is spent at the cheapest *sufficient* rung and that reaching the bottom rung is itself evidence the cheaper ones failed. The chapter puts a price on the bottom rung: 修櫓轒轀，具器械，三月而後成；距闉，又三月而後已 — three months to build the gear, three more to take the wall. The last line devalues even a perfect battle record, and names the best outcome as the one where no fight occurred.
- **Design rule**: When a workflow keeps failing, escalate in the written order: rewrite the plan → change the agent or the engine → add parallelism or rounds → do the work directly, and record which rung the run is on. Everything below the last rung is where the effort belongs; the last rung is permitted only when labelled as the admission that the cheaper rungs failed.
- **Violated by**: The third failed verification triggers "add four more reviewers and a longer loop", skipping the rung of rewriting the plan that was wrong.
- **Merge note**: 百戰百勝 and 不戰而屈人之兵 are one idea — both say the best result avoids the fight — so they are not separate principles. I also merged the repeated 以全爭於天下 into this rung ladder rather than giving "win whole" a principle of its own; it is the same cost argument seen from the other side.

### 3. Buy the information before you buy the commitment
- **Text**: 故兵貴勝，不貴久。…不知諸侯之謀者，不能豫交；不知山林、險阻、沮澤之形者，不能行軍；不用鄉導者，不能得地利。…先知者，不可取於鬼神，不可象於事，不可驗於度，必取於人，知敵之情者也。 — "War values victory, not duration. … He who does not know the neighbouring lords' designs cannot make alliances in advance; who does not know mountains, ravines, passes and marshes cannot march his army; who uses no local guide cannot gain the advantage of the ground. … Foreknowledge cannot be taken from ghosts or spirits, cannot be matched against omens, cannot be verified by calculation — it must be taken from men, from those who know the enemy's condition." (作戰第二; 軍爭第七; 用間第十三)
- **Basis**: VERIFIED (source: Wikisource 孫子兵法, fetched 2026-10-05)
- **Reading**: 兵貴勝，不貴久 is usually quoted as a slogan for speed, but in context it sits between two cost lists — a hundred thousand troops burning a thousand gold a day, and seven tenths of a household's means consumed. The chapter is about expense, and 久 is what expense buys by default. The three-part couplet then names what must be known *before* acting, and 用間 says where knowledge actually comes from: recruited people, not inference or divination.
- **Design rule**: Spend a bounded, named amount on reconnaissance — read the code, run the failing case, ask whoever built it — before spending the execution budget. If reconnaissance is not affordable, shrink the plan's scope and say so; do not execute blind at full size.
- **Violated by**: Six agents burn tokens reconstructing a plan from a one-paragraph request, and nobody opens the repository that would have settled the question in one read.
- **Merge note**: 兵貴勝，不貴久, the three-thing couplet, and 先知者…必取於人 are one idea at three scales — duration, preconditions, and the source of knowledge. I have given speed, reconnaissance and foreknowledge one principle between them rather than three thin ones.

### 4. Asymmetry: show a shape, keep the interior
- **Text**: 兵者，詭道也。…故兵以詐立，以利動，以分合為變者也。…故形人而我無形，則我專而敵分。 — "War is the way of deception. … In war the army stands on deception, moves on advantage, and takes its variation in splitting and joining. … By taking a form while I have none, I am concentrated and he is divided." (始計第一; 軍爭第七; 虛實第六)
- **Basis**: VERIFIED (source: Wikisource 孫子兵法, fetched 2026-10-05)
- **Reading**: 形人 is to take on a form the enemy can see; 我無形 is to have none he can see. The stated consequence is arithmetic, not mystique: a divided enemy is beatable ten-to-one because a divided enemy must prepare everywhere, and 備前則後寡 is the mechanism. 分合 — splitting and joining — is named as the *only* sanctioned variable of manoeuvre, with the other clauses (其疾如風, 其徐如林 …) describing tempo rather than doctrine.
- **Design rule**: A workflow publishes the shape of its investigation but not its blind spots, and it varies its shape only by splitting and merging nodes. If every node holds the whole design rationale from the start, the division was created by you and there is no concentrated force anywhere to hit.
- **Violated by**: Each subagent receives the full design rationale up front and returns a paraphrase of it, and the verifier is handed the same brief the workers were handed.
- **Loose**: "Hide state from your own collaborators" is *not* in this line. 形人 and 我無形 are about the enemy; 詐 in 軍爭 is a ruse used outward. Mapping it onto intra-team information hiding is my extension, and a workflow that hides its state from its own verifier is abusing the line rather than obeying it.

### 5. Set the ground, and make the other side come to you
- **Text**: 凡先處戰地而待敵者佚，後處戰地而趨戰者勞。故善戰者，致人而不致於人。能使敵自至者，利之也；能使敵不得至者，害之也…敵佚能勞之，飽能饑之，安能動之。 — "Whoever first occupies the ground and waits for the enemy is at ease; whoever comes later and hurries to the fight is exhausted. Hence the skilled general brings the enemy to him and is not brought to him. To make the enemy come to you is advantageous; to make him unable to come is dangerous. … If he is at ease, wear him; if he is full, starve him; if he is settled, make him move." (虛實第六)
- **Basis**: VERIFIED (source: Wikisource 孫子兵法, fetched 2026-10-05)
- **Reading**: 佚 and 勞 are rest and toil, and the whole chapter is about refusing to meet the enemy on terms of his choosing — the second half of the passage gives the three moves (wear the rested, starve the full, unsettle the settled). The verb 致 appears in the first clause and is negated in the second, and that pairing is the whole principle.
- **Design rule**: Choose the meeting ground, and require the task to arrive in a shape your workflow can process — the artifact, the file, the failing test, not a story. A node dragged into the requester's format is the 後處戰地 case, and it will be 勞: the cost lands as rework nobody budgeted.
- **Violated by**: The workflow accepts a vaguely scoped request and then spends most of its budget discovering what the deliverable is, instead of making the requester state it.
- **Ambiguous**: 致 is read either as "make come to me" (receive) or as "converge on / bring force against" (attack). The second is supported by the following line 使敵自至 and the first by the shape of 致人而不致於人. Both yield the same rule; neither should be quoted without the other half of the sentence.

### 6. No fixed form: the plan is a response shape, not a script
- **Text**: 故其戰勝不復，而應形於無窮。…故兵無成勢，無恒形，能因敵變化而取勝者，謂之神。…夫兵形象水，水之行，避高而趨下；兵之勝，避實而擊虛…水因地而制行，兵因敵而制勝。 — "So his victories are not repeated, but take the shape of whatever comes, without end. … War has no completed momentum and no constant form; to take victory by changing with the enemy is to be divine. … War is like water: the way of water is to avoid what is high and run to what is low; the way of victory is to avoid what is solid and strike what is hollow." (虛實第六)
- **Basis**: VERIFIED (source: Wikisource 孫子兵法, fetched 2026-10-05)
- **Reading**: 勝不復 means a victory that recurs identically is evidence of a system that has stopped adapting. 應形 is to match the incoming shape. 無成勢 denies there is a transferable momentum that carries between engagements, and 無恒形 denies a constant form. The water image is the chapter's own compression of the same claim, and it specifies *how*: avoid the solid, strike the hollow, and take your course from the ground you are given.
- **Design rule**: Fix the invariants, never the sequence. What a workflow must fix is the check, the evidence and the stop condition; the order and number of steps is free to change with what arrives. A design that names a fixed sequence has no 恒形 left to work with.
- **Violated by**: A pipeline whose stage 3 assumes the exact artefact stage 2 emits, so a well-formed but differently-shaped input silently dies at the join.
- **Loose**: 兵形象水 is the line everyone reaches for when they mean "be adaptive", and the Agile reading drops both halves — 避實而擊虛 and 水因地而制行. The text's version is "be shaped by this ground and hit what is thin here", which is a specific instruction, not a temperament.
- **Relation**: This is the internal counterpart of principle 4. There, what you show and what you hide; here, what you fix and what you vary.

### 7. Design the situation, not the person
- **Text**: 故善戰者，求之於勢，不責於人，故能擇人而任勢。任勢者，其戰人也，如轉木石。木石之性，安則靜，危則動，方則止，圓則行。…故善戰者之勝也，無智名，無勇功。 — "Hence the skilled general seeks his advantage in the momentum of the situation and does not blame the men; that is why he can choose the men and rely on the momentum. One who relies on momentum fights as if he were rolling wood and stone. The nature of wood and stone: at rest they are still, on a slope they move; square they stop, round they travel. … The skilled general's victories carry no name for cleverness and no credit for courage." (兵勢第五; 軍形第四)
- **Basis**: VERIFIED (source: Wikisource 孫子兵法, fetched 2026-10-05)
- **Reading**: 求之於勢 and 不責於人 are one move: put the result in the structure so that who executes it stops mattering. The wood and stone image says the same thing without metaphor — a log is not clever and a boulder is not brave, but set on a slope they move, and the position is the mechanism. 無智名, 無勇功 adds that a good general's win looks unremarkable and cannot be attributed to one brilliant action.
- **Design rule**: A workflow's quality is a property of the design, not of the best agent in it. If the result depends on catching a strong model in the right slot, the design has not taken responsibility for the outcome, and that is fixed before the next run rather than explained after the next failure.
- **Violated by**: A failing node is re-run with a stronger model and the run is called a success once the strong model happens to hold a line the design left open.
- **Merge note**: 無智名，無勇功 is the same design point from the winner's side, so it stays here rather than becoming a principle about invisible work.

### 8. Control the conditions, not the verdict
- **Text**: 昔之善戰者，先為不可勝，以待敵之可勝。不可勝在己，可勝在敵。故善戰者，能為不可勝，不能使敵之必可勝。故曰：勝可知，而不可為。…勝兵先勝而後求戰，敗兵先戰而後求勝。 — "The skilled generals of old first made themselves unbeatable and waited for the enemy to become beatable. The unbeatable lies with oneself; the beatable lies with the enemy. Hence the skilled general can make himself unbeatable; he cannot make the enemy certainly beatable. Thus it is said: victory can be known; it cannot be manufactured. … The army that wins makes its victory first and then seeks the battle; the army that loses seeks the battle first and then seeks victory." (軍形第四)
- **Basis**: VERIFIED (source: Wikisource 孫子兵法, fetched 2026-10-05)
- **Reading**: The division is explicit: 不可勝在己 is the half you own, 可勝在敵 the half you do not, and the second sentence is a promise on one side and a refusal on the other. The last sentence gives the operational meaning — a winning army builds the position first, then engages, which is the same order as building the check before arguing the conclusion.
- **Design rule**: Split every node's obligation into a part you can guarantee and a part you can only observe. A workflow may guarantee that evidence was gathered, that the check ran, and that the loop stopped; it may not guarantee the conclusion. The guaranteed half is what the artifact delivers.
- **Violated by**: The workflow's contract is "produce the correct answer", so a wrong answer and a workflow that ran the wrong check are indistinguishable to whoever reads the output.
- **Ambiguous**: 為 in 勝可知，而不可為 is read as "compel" (you may not compel a win) or as "manufacture" (victory is not a product of effort); the clause admits both. The same received text also carries a variant one chapter earlier in a related line — 勝可擅, glossed 一作「為」也 — so a third reading, "victory may be manoeuvred", is textually attested and I cannot say which the received text prefers. Also undecidable here: whether 勝 in 勝可知 is the noun (a knowable state) or the verb (winning, knowable as predictable).

### 9. No dispatch without a named gain
- **Text**: 非利不動，非得不用，非危不戰。主不可以怒而興師，將不可以慍而致戰。…合於利而動，不合於利而止。 — "Do not move without advantage; do not employ troops without obtaining; do not fight unless at risk. A ruler may not raise an army in anger; a general may not come to battle in resentment. … Move in accordance with advantage; halt when there is none." (火攻第十二; 九地第十一)
- **Basis**: VERIFIED (source: Wikisource 孫子兵法, fetched 2026-10-05)
- **Reading**: Three gates, each a "no", and the first is the operative one — the benefit must be defined *before* the movement. The anger clauses apply the same gate to a case everyone recognises: an emotional trigger is not a licensed reason to spend. The closing line, repeated at the end of 九地, makes halt a first-class action rather than a failure to act.
- **Design rule**: Every node in a design must name the state it changes and the check that will see the change. A node that cannot name both is not dispatched. A node whose check can pass either way is not a node, it is a ritual.
- **Violated by**: A parallel branch spawned "to be safe" that returns a summary nobody reads and whose failure blocks nothing.
- **Ambiguous**: 非得不用 — whether 得 is gain in the same sense as 利, or acquisition of the enemy's resources, is not decidable from the line. The workflow reading needs only the uncontested half, "no named gain, no dispatch".

### 10. Freshness is perishable; put the sharp unit where agreement is automatic
- **Text**: 三軍可奪氣，將軍可奪心。是故朝氣銳，晝氣惰，暮氣歸。故善用兵者，避其銳氣，擊其惰歸，此治氣者也；以治待亂，以靜待嘩，此治心者也；以近待遠，以佚待勞，以飽待饑，此治力者也…無邀正正之旗 out of the three armies and the heart out of the general. Therefore morning spirit is sharp, midday spirit slackens, evening spirit has gone home. Hence the skilled general avoids the sharp spirit and strikes the slack and the departed — that is managing spirit. Meet disorder with order and clamour with stillness — that is managing the heart. Meet the far with the near, the weary with the rested, the hungry with the full — that is managing force." (軍爭第七)
- **Basis**: VERIFIED (source: Wikisource 孫子兵法, fetched 2026-10-05)
- **Reading**: 氣 is morale and it decays on a clock: the same formation is worth less by evening. The rule is not "attack tired armies" as a tactic but *timing* — your unit's window of maximum value is not the same window as your opponent's, which is why 避其銳氣 and 擊其惰歸 are paired. The chapter's own arithmetic supports the reading: 百里而爭利 captures the generals and only a tenth of the force arrives, 五十里 loses half, 三十里 loses a third.
- **Design rule**: Treat a fresh context as the scarce resource, and schedule your strongest, most independent check where agreement is most likely to be automatic — the late node everyone expects to rubber-stamp — not at the front where attention is high and context is intact everywhere.
- **Violated by**: The one element that must be independent and sharp, the final verifier, is given the same agent that produced the work, immediately after it, with the same full context, as the last node of a long run.
- **Merge note**: 朝氣銳 / 晝氣惰 / 暮氣歸 and 以近待遠, 以佚待勞, 以飽待饑 are the same claim about freshness stated twice, so they are one principle.

### 11. Agreement on the purpose is a precondition, not a preamble
- **Text**: 道者，令民與上同意，可與之死，可與之生，而不畏危也。…故知勝有五：知可以戰與不可以戰者勝，識衆寡之用者勝，上下同欲者勝，以虞待不虞者勝，將能而君不御者勝。此五者，知勝之道也。 — "The Way is that the people below agree with the commander above, so that you can go with them to die and go with them to live, and they are not afraid of danger. … There are five ways of knowing victory: to know when you can fight and when you cannot; to know the use of many and few; upper and lower sharing one desire; to be prepared against the unprepared; a capable general who is not restrained by his ruler. These five are the way of knowing victory." (始計第一; 謀攻第三)
- **Basis**: VERIFIED (source: Wikisource 孫子兵法, fetched 2026-10-05)
- **Reading**: 同意 is literally the same intent, and the sentence measures it under load — 可與之死 is the extreme case, so "same purpose" means willingness under risk, not saying the same words. The five conditions are a readiness list: two about fit, one about alignment, one about preparation, one about authority. Four of the five are checkable before the fight, and none of them is about the opponent's skill.
- **Design rule**: A design is not ready until the objective is written once and every node's prompt traces back to it without reinterpretation, and until the fan-out number has a stated justification (識衆寡之用 — knowing when one is enough and when many are). Alignment is a gate, not a paragraph you hope the agents absorbed.
- **Violated by**: Six subagents receive six differently-worded restatements of the same goal, and the divergence surfaces at the merge step, where it is most expensive to detect.
- **Ambiguous**: 上下同欲 is usually "upper and lower share one desire", but 同欲 could equally be "the same wish" as distinct from shared *reason*; the line says the alignment must exist, not that it must be reasoned. A workflow can satisfy the letter of this with a slogan, and the text does not tell you how to avoid that.

### 12. Write down where the agents are allowed to disobey
- **Text**: 途有所不由，軍有所不擊，城有所不攻，地有所不爭，君命有所不受。…故將通於九變之利者，知用兵矣；將不通於九變之利，雖知地形，不能得地之利矣 not to be taken, armies not to be attacked, cities not to be assaulted, ground not to be contested, and the ruler's commands not to be obeyed. … The general who is versed in the advantages of the nine changes knows how to use troops." (九變第八)
- **Basis**: VERIFIED (source: Wikisource 孫子兵法, fetched 2026-10-05)
- **Reading**: This is the only place in the thirteen chapters where the sovereign is named as something to be disobeyed, and it is stated as a list of five *refusals*, the last being the largest. The text gives no criteria for the refusal, which is the honest part: the rule is that the authority to refuse exists, and that the ruler is the one who must not remove it. The surrounding sentence supplies the cost of getting it wrong — a general who does not know the nine changes, however well he knows the terrain, cannot get the advantage of the ground.
- **Design rule**: A design must name, in advance and in the document, the actions an agent may refuse and the actions it may not. "The agent should exercise judgement" is not a rule; "this node may skip a step that fails check X, and must not skip a step that fails check Y" is. If no node in the graph has a documented right of refusal, the graph has no flexibility, which is the 將不通於九變之利 case.
- **Violated by**: An executor node has no documented authority to skip a failed step, so it repairs the step or reports failure instead of routing around it, and the run burns a loop on a step both parties already know is wrong.
- **Ambiguous**: 君命有所不受 is read narrowly — only orders that are operationally ruinous may be refused — and widely, as a general holding judgement in all matters. Context supports the narrow reading; the absence of any stated condition in 不受 leaves the wide one open. I flag it because a workflow built on the wide reading can justify overriding the designer, which is not what the chapter is doing.

### 13. Coordinate like one body, and commit by contract rather than by explanation
- **Text**: 故善用兵者，譬如率然。率然者，常山之蛇也。擊其首則尾至，擊其尾則首至，擊其中則首尾俱至。…是故方馬埋輪，未足恃也；齊勇如一，政之道也；剛柔皆得，地之理也。…故善用兵者，攜手若使一人，不得已也。…犯三軍之衆，若使一人。犯之以事，勿告以言；犯之以利，勿告以害。 — "The skilled general is like a rattlesnake. A rattlesnake is the snake of Mount Chang: strike its head and the tail arrives; strike the tail and the head arrives; strike the middle and both ends arrive. … Therefore to harness the horses and bury the wheels is not yet to be relied on; to make their courage uniform as one is the way of good government. … The skilled general takes his men as though they were clasped hands. … Commit the mass of the three armies as though it were one man. Commit them by the task, and do not tell them the words; commit them by the interest, and do not tell them the danger." (九地第十一)
- **Basis**: VERIFIED (source: Wikisource 孫子兵法, fetched 2026-10-05)
- **Reading**: The snake image is response coordination — a hit anywhere produces a reaction everywhere, with no commander relaying it. 方馬埋輪，未足恃也 says the external constraints were bound and were still not enough, so the integration has to come from somewhere else. 齊勇如一, 政之道也 makes uniform effort a question of policy, not of morale. The closing clause is the sharp one: men are committed by task and by interest and are deliberately not told the reasoning or the risk.
- **Design rule**: Integrate a swarm through shared state that any node can read and write, and commit a node by its contract and its check, not by a paragraph of rationale. The integration lives in the interface, not in the briefing.
- **Violated by**: Nodes pass prose to each other and re-derive the task from prose, so a wave of work is joined by a translator instead of a state file, and a response to a hit at one end of the snake has to be relayed by hand.
- **Loose**: "Do not tell your agents the reasoning" is a hard reading of 勿告以言. The weaker and safer reading is that an agent's commitment comes from the task and the stake, not from a persuasive brief — which is an argument against motivational prompting, not against informing. A workflow that strips agents of the context they need will fail, and the text says nothing about that case.

## What I would not claim

**The "three stratagems" are not in this text.** 上畧伐智，中畧伐義，下畧伐勢 — "the highest stratagem attacks the mind, the middle attacks the alliance, the lowest attacks the position" — is quoted across the internet as Sun Tzu's 三十六計 counterpart. In the received text I read it does not appear in the thirteen chapters. It is in that edition's editorial appendix, attributed to a 《孫子兵法論》 as quoted by 《北堂書鈔》, and the editor immediately adds 按︰此亦不似孫武語 — "this too does not sound like Sun Wu's words", reasoning that later writers traced everything to Sun Wu and so wrote a 兵法論 and titled it as one. The same appendix notes that a neighbouring passage, 貴之而無驕，委之而不專，扶之而無隱，危之而不懼, is in 《太平御覽》 attributed to Zhuge Liang's 《兵要》. So "Sun Tzu's three stratagems" is a late attribution to a Sun Tzu *tradition*, not a line in Sun Tzu, and I would not build a design rule on it as a quotation.

**The 答話 is the weakest text in the edition.** The questions of the King of Wu and Sun Wu's answers — including the most quotable applied scenarios anyone remembers, such as what to do when the enemy is unready, entrenched, or dug in — appear after 用間, outside the thirteen chapters, in the same received edition. The edition's own note is not a clean verdict: it observes that a commentator 何氏 cited one of those answers as 兵法曰 and concluded from that that the dialogue *is* within the eighty-two chapters. That the attribution was already being argued when the edition was compiled is itself the checkable fact. So "Sun Tzu's answers to the King of Wu" is not something I can source to Sun Wu, and it is where a lot of invented quotation tends to live.

**「知彼知己，百戰不殆」 is not a guarantee of invincibility.** The full line gives a graded scale — 不知彼而知己，一勝一負; 不知彼不知己，每戰必殆 — and the same received text twice undercuts the maximal reading: it devalues 百戰百勝 as 非善之善者也, and it supplies a third term in 地形第十, where the formula is 知彼知己，勝乃不殆；知天知地，勝乃可全. The maxim is the top rung of a ranked list, with self-knowledge and knowledge of the enemy as necessary conditions and knowledge of the ground added on. The popular English "know yourself and your enemy and you cannot lose" drops the grading and the third term, and turns a ranking into a promise. Note also that 軍形 puts the control asymmetrically — 不可勝在己, 可勝在敵 — which is a narrower claim than the slogan and a more useful one.
